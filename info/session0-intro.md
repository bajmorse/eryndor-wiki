# Session 0 — Opening Introduction

*A summary of the three hundred years since the Eldressa Campaign, written for player orientation.*

---

## Previously...

It has been 300 years since the Void Rift was sealed and the Second Demon War ended. The sealing — known as the Unification — was a joint effort between mortals and Demons, and it held. The Demons who remained in Eryndor were offered terms rather than extermination, and most stayed, slowly integrating into Eldressan society over the generations that followed. The Dragons disappeared in the aftermath, but returned roughly 200 years ago — not as ancient stewards, but as partners, establishing the Dragon Rider network that now connects the continents. Valtiron was founded as a neutral diplomatic seat. Meridia grew into the largest city in the world and the center of global trade. Velkrand industrialized and is pushing toward political unification. The world is more connected than it has ever been.

---

It has been three hundred years since the Void Rift was sealed.

Three hundred years since the Second Demon War ground to its bitter end — since the last of those terrible rifts in the sky closed, and the killing stopped, and exhausted soldiers on both sides looked across the silence and wondered what came next. Three hundred years since the Unification: the moment mortals and Demons, ancient enemies shaped by a war that predated the mortal world itself, chose something other than extinction.

The world that came after is almost unrecognizable.

The Demons did not go home. Many had no home left — Gontkhael, their shattered world, was dead space and drifting rubble. They stayed, rebuilt alongside the peoples they had warred against, and slowly — generation by generation — became part of the fabric of Eryndor. Today, a Demon named Serazeth Vael sits as Chancellor of Valtiron, the most powerful diplomatic seat in the known world. That would have been unthinkable in the age your grandparents' grandparents were born into.

And the Dragons came back.

No one fully understands where they went, or why, or what they sacrificed during those centuries of silence. What is known is that roughly two hundred years ago they returned — not as stewards or rulers, but as something new. Dragon Riders now carry messages across oceans in days. Continents that once lived and died without knowing each other's names now trade, argue, and negotiate as a matter of course.

Meridia rose from a backwater fishing port to the largest city in the known world — the place where every major power comes to do business, because every major power needs it to function. The Bank of Unification prints the coin that moves the global economy.

Velkrand, once a continent of warring clans and ash-buried cities, is closer to political unification than it has ever been. The forges never stop. The rails keep spreading. The artificers keep building things no one thought was possible.

And Eldressa — old, complicated, proud Eldressa — remains what it has always been: the political and cultural heart of the known world, held together by diplomacy and history rather than any single crown.

Three hundred years of relative peace. The longest the world has known.

But peace is not the same as resolution. Ancient ruins still hold things that should stay buried. Old debts between peoples are not forgiven just because the wars that created them are over. Forces that predate any living institution are still moving in the dark.

The world is more connected than it has ever been.

Which means, when something goes wrong, it goes wrong everywhere.
