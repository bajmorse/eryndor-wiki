# Canon Questions of Eryndor

*Version 0.1 — Active Development Topics*

---

# Purpose

This document tracks unresolved or partially-defined areas of Eryndor canon.

These topics represent foundational lore questions that will shape future worldbuilding decisions, history articles, campaign arcs, races, religions, and political structures.

Each section may eventually graduate into fully canonical encyclopedia articles once finalized.

---

# Priority 1 — Foundations of the Setting

These questions define the cosmology and mythological backbone of Eryndor.

---

# 1. The Celestial War

**Status:** Completed

Known Facts:

* Angels existed before mortal civilization.
* Devils existed before mortal civilization.
* Dragons acted as cosmic balance keepers.
* The Dragons failed in their duty.
* A catastrophic war erupted between Angels and Devils.
* The war ultimately led to the destruction of worlds.

Unresolved Questions:

* Why did the war begin?
* What ideological conflict existed between Angels and Devils?
* Were either side morally correct?
* What exactly caused the Dragons to fail?
* Was the war inevitable?
* Did the Dragons choose neutrality, isolation, or inaction?

Narrative Importance:

The Celestial War serves as the mythological foundation of the setting and may become Eryndor's equivalent of a "War in Heaven" mythology.

---

# 2. The Shattering

**Status:** Working Canon

Known Facts:

* Multiple worlds once existed.
* The world of Gontkhael was destroyed.
* Eryndor survived.
* The Shattering permanently altered reality.

Unresolved Questions:

* How many worlds existed?
* Was Eryndor damaged during the Shattering?
* What exactly happened during the destruction of Gontkhael?
* Are remnants of the old worlds still accessible?
* Did the Shattering create the Void?
* Did the Shattering alter magic itself?

Narrative Importance:

The Shattering determines the cosmology of the setting and the true nature of the universe.

---

# 3. The Fall of the Angels

**Status:** Working Canon

Known Facts:

* The Angels discovered and used the Soulstone of Gontkhael.
* The Soulstone shattered.
* The Angels were transformed into Demons.
* The surviving Demons fled or became trapped outside their dying world.

Unresolved Questions:

* Did all Angels become Demons?
* Did any Angels survive unchanged?
* Are true Angels extinct?
* Can a Demon restore their soul?
* Is the "White Demon" legend true?
* What role does the black blood prophecy play in modern Demon society?

Narrative Importance:

This event defines the origin and tragedy of Demonkind.

---

# Priority 2 — The Great Historical Events

These questions define the major turning points of recorded history.

---

# 4. The First Demon War

**Status:** Under Revision

Older Historical Version:

* Drow opened a portal.
* Demons invaded Eldressa.
* Dragons sacrificed themselves to stop the invasion.
* The Great Mountain was destroyed.

Modern Canon Direction:

* Demons are not inherently evil.
* Devils may have manipulated the conflict.
* History may have incorrectly blamed Demonkind.

Unresolved Questions:

* What truly caused the war?
* Who opened the portal?
* Were Demons refugees, invaders, or both?
* What role did the Devils play?
* Was history intentionally rewritten?
* Did mortal races misunderstand the conflict?

Narrative Importance:

This may become the defining moral tragedy of the setting.

---

# 5. The Dragon Sacrifice

**Status:** Working Canon

Known Facts:

* Dragons disappeared for centuries.
* The Great Mountain was shattered.
* Valtiron was eventually built around the ruins.

Unresolved Questions:

* What exactly did the Dragons sacrifice?
* Did they die?
* Did they seal something away?
* Did they retreat intentionally?
* Why did they vanish for so long?
* Why have they returned now?
* Did all Dragons participate equally?

Narrative Importance:

The Dragon Sacrifice defines the transition from the mythic age into the modern age.

---

# 6. The Second Demon War

**Status:** Undefined

Known Facts:

* The modern world was shaped by a more recent great conflict.
* Dragons eventually returned afterward.
* Modern politics emerged from its aftermath.

Unresolved Questions:

* Who fought in the war?
* What caused it?
* What role did Demons play?
* What role did Devils play?
* What truths were revealed?
* How recent was the conflict?
* What directly led to the modern political era?

Narrative Importance:

This event likely serves as the bridge between ancient history and the modern world.

---

# Priority 3 — Modern Political Structure

These questions shape the modern geopolitical landscape.

---

# 7. Valtiron

**Status:** Working Canon

Known Facts:

* Valtiron surrounds the ruins of the shattered Great Mountain.
* It hosts continental councils and diplomatic assemblies.
* It is politically important but not an empire capital.

Unresolved Questions:

* Is Valtiron an independent city-state?
* Who governs the city?
* Is it jointly administered?
* Does it possess military neutrality?
* How much influence do Dragons possess there?
* Is it symbolic, political, or both?

Comparative Inspirations:

* Geneva
* Brussels
* Constantinople
* Washington D.C.

---

# 8. Velkrand Consolidation

**Status:** Working Canon

Known Facts:

* Velkrand remains politically fragmented.
* Industrial consortiums and artificer guilds hold immense power.
* Continental consolidation is underway.

Unresolved Questions:

* Does a formal continental union exist?
* Is the consolidation peaceful?
* Who opposes unification?
* Are the coastal free cities resisting?
* Is industrialization causing instability?
* How centralized is the emerging authority?

Narrative Importance:

Velkrand may become the first truly modern superpower.

---

# 9. Meridia and Nadris

**Status:** Working Canon

Known Facts:

* Meridia is the diplomatic and commercial center of the known world.
* Nadris serves as a haven for illicit commerce and privateering.
* The two are economically intertwined.

Unresolved Questions:

* Are they technically separate cities?
* Are they rivals?
* Is corruption openly tolerated?
* Does Meridia secretly rely upon Nadris?
* Are they politically connected behind closed doors?

Narrative Importance:

Together they embody the moral ambiguity of global commerce.

---

# Priority 4 — Spiritual and Religious Questions

These define the metaphysical flavor of the world.

---

# 10. The Great Beasts of the Heartwood

**Status:** Working Canon

Known Facts:

* The Great Beasts hold immense spiritual influence within Ilyndor.
* The Heartwood is considered sacred.
* Their authority is respected by many peoples.

Unresolved Questions:

* Are the Great Beasts divine?
* Are they ancient Deonati?
* Are they emissaries of the Beastlands?
* Are they immortal?
* How directly do they intervene in mortal affairs?

Narrative Importance:

They form the spiritual backbone of Ilyndor.

---

# 11. Religion in the Modern Era

**Status:** Undefined

Known Facts:

* Different peoples possess different creation myths.
* Ancient cosmological events were partially real.
* Modern scholarship has rediscovered fragments of lost truth.

Unresolved Questions:

* Which religions are actually correct?
* Are all creation myths partially true?
* How much do modern people know about the Celestial War?
* Are the Gods active?
* Are religions divided by race, region, or ideology?
* How has the return of Dragons affected religion?

Narrative Importance:

Religion may become one of the most politically destabilizing forces in the modern age.
