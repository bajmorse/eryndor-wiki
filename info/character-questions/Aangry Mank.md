# Aangry Mank — Questions

Simiah Beastbound Ranger, native of Sky-Bough Village. Bonded to [[Buffa-lo Bill]]. Grizzled, white-maned, glowing blue tattoos across scalp and forearm, feathers and beads at wrist and belt.

Of the five, he's the one with the deepest roots in the village — so his questions lean toward *what home actually looks like* rather than how he found it.

## Roots in Sky-Bough

- Born and raised in Sky-Bough itself, or did he grow up somewhere nearby? Does he have a home of his own in the village — and if so, what's it like?
- Does he have family still living in Sky-Bough — parents, siblings, extended kin? Are they part of the Simiah community there, or something else?
- Is he embedded in Sky-Bough's Simiah population, or does he keep more to himself?
- What's his actual trade or role in the village — how does he spend his days and make his living? (He's not an adventurer yet — this is who he is before any of that starts.)

## Personal history

- What's the story behind the tattoos — a rite of passage, a personal marker, something spiritual? Do they mean something specific, or did they just accumulate over time?
- The feathers and beads at his wrists and belt — gifts, trophies, ritual items, something he's added to over the years?
- Where does the name "Aangry" come from — is it what he was given, a nickname that stuck, or something else entirely?

## Buffa-lo Bill

- How did the bond with [[Buffa-lo Bill]] form — when, where, and why a buffalo specifically? Is an albino buffalo companion unusual for a Beastbound Ranger, or common where he trained?
- Anyone else ever tried to separate them, or is the pair just an accepted fact of life in Sky-Bough at this point?

## The village's troubles

- Has he ever fought in the [[The Sky-Bough Martial Arts Tournament]] — win, lose, or never bothered? What's his read on the rumors that it's rigged?
- What's his personal read on the mayor, and on Joe? Does being a native give him a different stake in the ale shortage than the others have?
