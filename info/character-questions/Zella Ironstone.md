# Zella Ironstone — Questions

Half-Infernis, half-Giant Lycan Blood Hunter, born in the Frostfang Mountains. Wanders Ilyndor extensively and returns to Sky-Bough whenever her travels bring her close by. Pale blue skin, curling horns, red hair in braids, scarred fur-trimmed armor, wolf-hilted swords.

She has the most unusual heritage of the five (an Infernis/Ice Giant pairing) and the most physically dangerous class combination (Lycan + Blood Hunter) — plenty of room to dig into origin, not just itinerary.

## Parentage and upbringing

- How did an Infernis and an Ice Giant end up together in the Frostfang Mountains? Is that still an ongoing relationship, or something in the past?
- Who did she actually grow up with — both parents together, within an Ice Giant settlement, within an Infernis village, or did the two of them raise her apart from either community given how unconventional the pairing was?
- What was it like growing up half-Infernis, half-Giant there — accepted as one of the community, treated as an outsider, something in between?
- Are either of her parents still alive? Any relationship with them now?

## Lycan and Blood Hunter

- Is she fully a werewolf, or does she have partial, controlled access to a Lycan hybrid form without being fully changed? Worth clarifying since lycanthropy isn't hereditary in this world — whatever happened to her, it wasn't simply passed down from a parent.
- However it actually happened — bitten, cursed, granted, sought out deliberately — what's the real story? This one matters.
- Who trained her as a Blood Hunter, and what does she actually hunt? Is this a professional calling, a personal vendetta, or something tied directly to being a Lycan herself?
- The wolf-hilted swords — family heirlooms, commissioned, taken as trophies from something she hunted?

## On the road, and in Sky-Bough

- What does her travel pattern actually look like — is there a rough circuit, or does she go wherever the hunt takes her?
- Out of everywhere she passes through, what draws her back to Sky-Bough specifically?
- How did a mountain-born wanderer end up part of a Sky-Bough friend group in the first place — what was the first meeting like?
