# Frayne Castel — Questions

Human Slayer. His party page currently says he's "from Goldhorn Crossing," a settlement destroyed in a territorial conflict that killed his family — **but Goldhorn Crossing is still standing.** He actually grew up in a smaller village near it, and that village is what was destroyed. Party Info and the Session 0 notes need updating to match once we've nailed this down, including a name for the destroyed village. Dark leather and chainmail, white skull painted across his chest, hand crossbow and long dagger.

This is the heaviest backstory of the five and already has real narrative weight — go gently, and let him decide how much to open up.

## Home village

- What was his home village actually like before it was destroyed — a farming hamlet, a trade waypoint, something else? What was its relationship to Goldhorn Crossing itself — dependent on it, just a close neighbor, something else? What was it called?
- Who exactly did he lose — parents, siblings, a partner, children? How old was he when it happened?
- What was "the territorial conflict" actually about, and who was fighting whom? Does he know, or was he too young / too far from the politics of it at the time?

## The years between

- Where did the wandering take him? Anywhere that left a real mark — a place, a person, a job that changed him?
- Did he travel alone the whole time, or fall in with anyone along the way?
- What kept him going during those years — anger, grief, something to prove, nothing in particular? Was it a slow drift, or was he actively running from something the whole time?

## Landing in Sky-Bough

- What tipped Sky-Bough from "another stop" into the place he actually stayed? Was there a specific moment, or did it just accumulate?
- The white skull painted on his chest — a personal symbol, a mourning ritual, something borrowed from somewhere he passed through?
- Has he ever learned who was actually responsible for his home village's destruction? Is that resolved for him, or still an open wound driving him?

## With the others

- How much of this does the rest of the friend group actually know? Is he open about it with them, or is this the first any of them are hearing the fuller version?
