# Finnegan Endercast — Questions

Infernis War Wizard, longtime resident of Sky-Bough (not native). Violet skin, gold-banded horns, dark hair, spellbook and scrollcase, deep red and black robes.

He's lived in Sky-Bough long enough to call it home "in truth, if not by birth" — so the open thread is where he came from, and what closed the door behind him.

## Before Sky-Bough

- Was he born on Ilyndor, or elsewhere? If Ilyndor, roughly where? If another continent, which one — Eldressa, Velkrand, Skeldran, Hrothan, the Meridian Isles?
- Did he grow up among other Infernis, or was his community mostly other peoples? If the latter, how was he treated growing up there — accepted, tolerated, something harder?
- How long has he actually lived in Sky-Bough, and what brought him there in the first place — did he choose it, or land there some other way?
- "War Wizard" implies training and study aimed at war, not having fought one. Whose conflict was he preparing for, and how far did that training take him before it fell away — a war that never came, one he trained for but ultimately walked from, something else?

## Craft and trappings

- The gold-banded horns — decorative, ceremonial, or does the banding actually do something (channel, focus, mark rank)?
- His spellbook and scrollcase — self-written and added to over years, inherited from a teacher, or something he acquired another way?
- What does he actually do for a living in Sky-Bough day to day — tutor, hired wand for hire, something unrelated to magic entirely?

## Family and identity

- Does he have any living family? Any contact with them, or is that a closed chapter?
- Infernis carry a complicated inheritance — does that history mean anything to him personally, or is it mostly other people's baggage he has to deal with?
- What made Sky-Bough specifically become home, rather than just the last place he stopped?
