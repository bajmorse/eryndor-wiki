# Session Prep — Character Questions

Working document, not published to either site. One file per player character, holding open-ended questions to ask before the next session — aimed at fleshing out backstory, personality, and each character's place in Sky-Bough Village and the wider world.

These are prompts for conversation, not a form to fill out. Pick whichever land best for a given player rather than running through the whole list.

The campaign starts at level 0: the five are not an adventuring party yet, just friends who drink together at the Bent Feather. Questions should ask about their lives and livelihoods as they are now, not frame anything as "between adventures." Also avoid inventing hyper-specific geography (named neighbourhoods, trunks, platforms) that isn't established anywhere — ask open questions instead and let the player fill in the detail.

## Group & Friendship (ask any of them)

The five aren't strangers thrown together — they're old friends and regular drinking companions who've been gathering at the Bent Feather for a while. Worth asking someone (doesn't have to be everyone):

- How far back does the friend group go? Who met whom first, and how did the other three fold in?
- Is there a "usual" dynamic at the table — who teases whom, who's the planner, who's always late?
- Aangry and Finnegan both call Sky-Bough home; Frayne stopped running there; Nyx and Zella pass through on a circuit. Does the group ever feel lopsided between the "locals" and the "regulars," or does nobody really think about it that way?
- Has the group ever been split up by real danger before, or has it mostly been travel, trade, and tavern nights so far?
- Do any two of them have a relationship the others don't fully know about — a debt, a past falling-out, something romantic, a promise made?
