# Nyx Ashwisk — Questions

Katari Hedge Witch and apothecary, traveling with a nomadic Katari band that circles the Heartwood. Regularly passes through Sky-Bough — one of her favourite stops, partly for the company and substantially for the Skybound Pale Ale. Bonded to her ferret familiar, [[Snowberry]].

She's the one whose "home" isn't a place at all — the questions lean into what the nomadic life looks like and why Sky-Bough stands out from everywhere else on her circuit.

## The band

- What's her nomadic Katari band actually like — family within it, elders, a leader? How does the band relate to the Heartwood and the Woodland Guardians?
- Is the band mainly Katari, or a mix of ancestries traveling together?
- Did she grow up in this band, or join it at some point?
- Is the band's circuit fixed, or does it shift year to year? Where else does it take her besides the Sky-Bough area?

## Craft

- How did she become a hedge witch — trained within the band, apprenticed to someone outside it, mostly self-taught?
- What's her actual specialty as an apothecary — herbalism, potions, remedies for something specific? Does she sell or trade her work in Sky-Bough, or is that separate from her visits?
- How did she and [[Snowberry]] bond?

## Sky-Bough specifically

- Of everywhere her circuit takes her, what makes Sky-Bough a favourite rather than just another stop? Company and ale are the official answer — is there a specific person (or people) she comes back for?
- Given her trade, has she ever worked with water from the Sacred Spring, or does she know anything about its properties from an apothecary's perspective?
- How long has she known this friend group, and how did a traveling witch end up regular company with four people who are mostly settled or local?
