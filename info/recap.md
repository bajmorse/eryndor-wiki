# Campaign Recap — The Sealing of the Void Rift

*A summary of the known events surrounding the end of the Second Demon War and the Unification.*

---

The exact details of the Second Demon War remain among the most debated and poorly documented periods in modern history. What follows is the accepted account as recorded by the historians of the Council of Valtiron.

---

## The Void Rift

At some point during the Second Demon War, a rift to the Void was opened somewhere within Eldressa. The precise cause — whether through deliberate ritual, catastrophic magical failure, or the intervention of forces still unidentified — is not known with certainty.

The Void, the endless expanse of cosmic darkness surrounding the Mortal Plane, had been dangerously close to the boundary of reality ever since the Shattering. When Gontkhael was destroyed and Nythorak banished, the barriers separating the Void from the mortal world were permanently weakened. The Rift represented the culmination of that ancient damage — a wound in reality large enough to threaten the stability of Eryndor itself.

Through the Rift came things that had no name in any mortal tongue. The conflict surrounding its existence became the defining catastrophe of the age.

---

## The Sealing

The Void Rift was sealed. The manner of its sealing is not fully recorded, or not fully disclosed.

What is known is that the sealing required a sacrifice — of power, of lives, or of something more difficult to name. The Dragons, who had served as stewards and guardians of Eryndor since the age of mortal civilization, disappeared from the world in the aftermath. Whether they gave themselves to close the Rift, or withdrew by their own choice, or were changed in some way that made them unable to remain, has never been authoritatively answered.

The sealing of the Void Rift is the event from which modern calendars measure time. It is called simply **the Unification** — not because the world was unified in any political sense, but because the act of closing the Rift required cooperation between peoples who had spent the war trying to destroy one another.

---

## The Peace

The Unification brought not only the end of the Void Rift but a formal end to the war between mortals and Demonkind.

The Demons who had entered Eryndor through the conflict were offered terms rather than extermination. Many chose to remain. The world was exhausted, devastated, and unwilling to continue fighting. The peace that followed was fragile, resentful in many quarters, and built on necessity as much as goodwill.

Three hundred years later, it has held.

---

## What Was Lost

The immediate aftermath of the Unification was defined as much by absence as by survival.

The Dragons were gone. Cities across Eldressa had been damaged or destroyed. The political structures of the pre-war era did not survive intact. Ancient knowledge, institutions, and lineages that had endured for centuries were simply gone.

The first generations of the Modern Age inherited a world that had survived, but only just.

---

## What Came After

The sealing of the Void Rift and the silence that followed it eventually gave way to something unexpected: recovery.

The Reconstruction Era. The Age of Reconnection. The return of the Dragons. The rise of Meridia. The founding of Valtiron in the crater of the old Great Mountain — peace built, deliberately, on the most visible scar of a previous catastrophe.

That history belongs to the three centuries since. What matters here is the moment it began: a rift closed, a war ended, and a world left to figure out what it meant to still be standing.
