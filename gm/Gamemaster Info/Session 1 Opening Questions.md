# Session 1 Opening Questions

A facilitation tool for opening Session 1: rather than the GM deciding which plot hook connects to which player character, these questions let the party discover and declare those connections themselves, live at the table.

## How to run this

Narrate an ordinary morning/day in Sky-Bough, then work through the questions below in randomized order (shuffle them, don't run top to bottom) — ask each one to the whole table at once, not to individual players by name. Whoever has an answer speaks up; let a short pause and one "anyone else?" happen before moving on, so a fast talker doesn't claim everything before quieter players get a beat to think.

Silence is a valid, complete answer. Nobody needs to say "no, I don't have a connection to this one" out loud — if nobody bites, that hook simply stays impersonal background for now, and that's genuine, useful information about where the party's attention naturally sits. Don't press for a connection just to make sure every hook lands somewhere.

Not every hook needs to surface in Session 1. Whichever ones the party does connect to are what shape the Tier 1 opening; the rest keep developing in the world regardless — see [[Gamemaster Info]] for the full list of storylines and how each thread progresses on its own.

## The Sky-Bough Martial Arts Tournament

- Is there someone in this year's tournament you're rooting for, betting on, or dreading watching lose?
- Have you ever entered yourself? How'd that go?

## The Ancient Pact / the lottery

- Do you know anyone who's been chosen by the lottery over the years — you, a friend, a friend's family? How did that sit with you?
- Where were you the night last year's sacrifice escaped, and what do you remember hearing about it?

## The Sea People

- Have you ever dealt with the Sea People directly, or know someone who has — trade, a warning, a close call, a story secondhand?

## The Missing Children

- Do you know any of the families who've lost a child? Close enough that it's personal, or just village-small-enough that everyone feels it?
- Has anyone close to you started acting strangely lately — quieter, avoidant, not quite themselves?

## The Forgotten Hero and the Tomb

- Have you heard the old stories about ruins or old bones turning up in the forest? Ever poked around anything like that yourself?

## Sir Testalot and the Testers

- Where do you personally land on the Testers — true believer, harmless weirdos, actively annoying?

## Political tension (the growing number of outsiders)

- Has the growing number of outsiders in town changed anything for you personally — a job, a friendship, a grudge?

## Skybound Pale Ale / The Sacred Spring

- How has the ale shortage actually hit you — do you drink at the Bent Feather, work there, sell to it, or just hear about it secondhand?
