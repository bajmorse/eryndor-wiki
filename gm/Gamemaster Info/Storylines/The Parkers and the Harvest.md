# The Parkers and the Harvest

Connected to [[Full Session Notes]] in the player wiki.

The Parkers don't need to become quest-givers — they're much more valuable as evidence that something is changing. Everyone knows the Parkers always have pastries, so when they suddenly don't, people notice.

## Current State

The Parkers, a Katari family of bakers beloved throughout Sky-Bough, are running short of flour, fruit, nuts, grain, and other staples from the farms surrounding the village. It's the first visible consequence of something considerably darker.

## Established GM Truths

- The poor harvest traces directly and solely to last year's failed sacrifice — the same weakening wards responsible for the receding Sacred Spring. See [[The Ancient Pact]].
- Farmers first noticed something wrong months after the failed sacrifice, consistent with how long the wards have taken to visibly weaken elsewhere. See [[The Ancient Pact]].
- What's happening to the crops is genuinely a mixture, not one clean symptom. Without the annual ritual renewing the wards that bind the Fae, its power has begun leaking out uncontrolled — centuries of pent-up rage and grief from its captivity bleeding into the surrounding land. Soil is turning infertile as that hatred seeps into it, and the same leaking anger is behind the rising aggression seen in local wildlife (see [[The Ancient Pact]]). The leak isn't uniform or targeted, which is why different farms and crops show different symptoms rather than one consistent failure.
- The Parkers source their ingredients from nearby farms, not travelling merchants or foraging — despite growing into something of a pastry conglomerate, they've stayed committed to buying local. Sky-Bough's farms sit in the surrounding area near the forest floor below the village.
- There's no clean order to which ingredients ran short first — flour, fruit, nuts, and grain all started thinning out at roughly the same time. That lack of a gradual, predictable pattern is part of what makes this year feel so wrong to farmers used to ordinary bad-harvest years.
- The poor harvest is uniformly bad — no farm is spared entirely — but it follows a gradient centered on the ritual site where the Fae is bound: worst close in, tapering off with distance, out to roughly a 100km radius in total.
- Wild flora within that same radius are affected too, but noticeably less than cultivated crops — farmed plants are less naturally compatible with the local soil to begin with, and the leaking rage seems to read cultivation itself as something invasive, hitting it harder than it hits what was already growing there naturally.
- Sky-Bough is well-managed enough to have stores comfortably bridging to the next harvest, plus a modest surplus beyond that — not thin enough to cause worry in an ordinary year, but nowhere near enough to weather a second failed harvest in a row.
- Dealing directly with so many farms across the affected area, the Parkers were among the first to notice the gradient pattern — worse close to the forest, tapering off with distance — and reported it to the mayor.
