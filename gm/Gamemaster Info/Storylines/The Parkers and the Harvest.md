# The Parkers and the Harvest

Connected to [[Full Session Notes]] in the player wiki.

The Parkers don't need to become quest-givers — they're much more valuable as evidence that something is changing. Everyone knows the Parkers always have pastries, so when they suddenly don't, people notice.

## Current State

The Parkers, a Katari family of bakers beloved throughout Sky-Bough, are running short of flour, fruit, nuts, grain, and other staples from the farms surrounding the village. It's the first visible consequence of something considerably darker.

## What the Party Knows

Everything under Current State. See [[Full Session Notes]].

## Rumours / What NPCs Believe

There's a widely repeated (false) story that the Parkers possess some ancient secret family recipe. In truth they're just extremely generous people, now caught out by a failing harvest.

## Open Questions

- Where do the Parkers normally get their ingredients?
- Are there farms on the forest floor, surrounding settlements, or travelling merchants?
- Which ingredients have become scarce first?
- How widespread is the poor harvest?
- Are all farms affected equally?
- Is there somewhere inexplicably not experiencing crop failure?
- When did farmers first notice something wrong?
- Does that timing correspond to the failed sacrifice?
- What exactly is happening to crops? Poor germination? Disease? Animals eating them? Soil becoming infertile?
- Are wild plants experiencing the same problem?
- How much food does Sky-Bough currently have stored?
- What happens if the next harvest fails completely?
- Do the Parkers know something unusual because they deal directly with so many suppliers?
