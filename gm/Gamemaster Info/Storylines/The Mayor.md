# The Mayor

Connected to [[Full Session Notes]] in the player wiki.

Avoid deciding she's secretly corrupt unless something emerging during play pushes that way — a well-intentioned mayor making questionable decisions is considerably richer.

## Current State

The mayor is a young Kenku woman, energetic and ambitious, particularly popular among Sky-Bough's younger inhabitants. She controls the commercial distribution of [[Skybound Pale Ale]] and has been cultivating relationships with merchants, travellers, and political contacts from beyond Ilyndor, quietly prioritizing dwindling supply for them.

## Rumours / What NPCs Believe

Older residents increasingly see her as selling pieces of Sky-Bough to outsiders, and the ale shortage has made that resentment much worse.

## Open Questions

- What is her name?
- How did someone so young become mayor?

- What does she want Sky-Bough to become?
- How does she feel about the Ancient Pact?
- Has she ever witnessed a sacrifice?
- What did she do when last year's sacrifice escaped?
- Does she secretly consider the escape a good thing?
- What does she know about the declining harvest?
- What does she know about the Sacred Spring?
- Who are the foreigners she's been inviting into Sky-Bough?
- Why does she believe those relationships are important enough to prioritize scarce Skybound for them?
- What recent incidents have occurred between those visitors and locals?
- Does she realize how much resentment is building?
- What is she currently hiding — not because she's evil, but because she thinks revealing it would make things worse?
