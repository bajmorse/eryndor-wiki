# The Sea People

Connected to [[Full Session Notes]] in the player wiki.

Probably the biggest blank canvas the players handed us. Don't start with "why are the Sea People attacking?" — start with "what happened that made people who have lived along the Summering Sea for generations suddenly change their behaviour?"

## Current State

"Sea People" isn't an actual name — it's simply what frightened inland villagers call them. They're a maritime culture living along the Summering Sea, primarily Sea Elves alongside other coastal peoples, and for generations their relationship with inland Ilyndor has been distant but peaceful. Recently, coastal fishing settlements have been attacked, fishing grounds have become unreliable, strange creatures are appearing, and some coastal communities have been abandoned. Armed bands have begun raiding inland settlements, but they aren't an organized invasion.

## Established GM Truths

- The escaped sacrifice did not flee toward the Summering Sea. The belief that they did is a baseless Tester rumour with no basis in fact. See [[Sir Testalot and the Testers]].
- Geography: the Gilded Sea is the entire western ocean, touching both Velkrand's and Ilyndor's west coasts. The Summering Sea is just the portion of it along Sky-Bough's coast. The **Sunreach Strait** is the passage between Ilyndor's north coast and Velkrand where the Warming Current runs.
- "The Sea People" aren't one culture — they're a mass wave of displaced coastal peoples pushed south and west down the Gilded Sea: off Velkrand's industrializing coast, and out of the Meridia/Eldressa colonial frontier expanding along northern Ilyndor (Coralspire, Mistwash Port, Seabridge, Tideward). They funnel through the Sunreach Strait and down the coast — the raids hitting Sky-Bough are the tail end of a much larger crisis, not its origin.
- What's changed is upstream, not local: industrial expansion in Velkrand and colonial expansion from Meridia/Eldressa have been displacing coastal communities for a while; Sky-Bough is only now feeling it as the wave reaches this far down the coast.
- The attackers are genuinely a mix of refugees, opportunistic raiders, and the desperate — no unifying culture, leadership, or shared name. "Sea People" is entirely a label frightened inland villagers invented.
- What they're afraid of is losing home and livelihood to forces they can't fight — nothing supernatural, no known tie to the Ancient Pact.
- The displaced wave is a genuine mix, which is part of why it can't be read as one culture: Sea Elves native to the northern islands, Deonati (particularly water-based subraces), and Galapa make up the bulk of it, alongside smaller numbers of other Ilyndoran peoples — Katari, Faun, Eladrin, Ribbet, Simiah — pushed out of the northern frontier. From Velkrand's industrializing coast come Dwarves, Gnomes, Goblins, and Humans, thrown into a maritime refugee crisis they have no cultural context for.
- Their historical relationship with inland Ilyndor (Sky-Bough included) has been distant but peaceful for generations — see Current State.
- The original Summering Sea coastal peoples still occupy most of their own villages and towns — this isn't a total collapse yet, but more are falling as the Sea People's advance continues.
- The Sea People themselves have no permanent home — mostly constant movement, with only loosely semi-stable waystation camps along the way. Their most recent waystation before reaching the Summering Sea was the Sunpetal Atolls, in western/northwestern Ilyndor.
- They have no official name, but among themselves have adopted **the Stormswept** — evocative both of the violence that forced them out and of being carried onward now by currents beyond their control.
- The outsiders increasingly visiting Sky-Bough are unrelated to the Stormswept crisis — they're the younger travelers and merchants drawn by [[Skybound Pale Ale]]'s trade growth and the mayor's own outreach. See [[The Mayor]].
- The Ancient Pact is not widely known even within Sky-Bough — it's deliberately guarded knowledge, restricted to those native to the village. The Stormswept know nothing about it. See [[The Ancient Pact]].

## Rumours / What NPCs Believe

The simple version making the rounds inland is "the Sea People are attacking us." Some believe they've violated the Ancient Pact; others believe they're connected to the escaped sacrifice. Some raiders are refugees, some are raiders in truth, and some are looking for something — possibly the escaped sacrifice.
