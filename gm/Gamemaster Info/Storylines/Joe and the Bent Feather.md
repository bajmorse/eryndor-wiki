# Joe and the Bent Feather

Connected to [[Full Session Notes]] in the player wiki.

Joe has emerged from Session 0 as a surprisingly important NPC.

## Current State

Joe is the longtime barkeep and brewmaster of the Bent Feather Tavern, and brewer of [[Skybound Pale Ale]]. He also organizes and hosts Sky-Bough's monthly [[The Sky-Bough Martial Arts Tournament|martial arts tournament]] — a separate affair from the tavern, despite sharing a host.

## Established GM Truths

- The Bent Feather predates Joe. He was born and raised in Sky-Bough, and his family was close with the previous proprietor.
- Joe left the village for a number of years before returning when the previous proprietor died. The tavern passed to him rather than to anyone with a stronger claim — why is not yet established (see Open Questions).
- As a child he was around the tavern but never formally trained as a brewer. His actual brewing skill comes from his years away, gathered from techniques of various lands and peoples — the Skybound recipe likely reflects real changes or additions he made, not an inheritance passed down unaltered.
- Joe and the mayor have a genuinely good relationship. He has voluntarily ceded the commercial side of Skybound to her because he has no interest in it — not because he is being pressured, exploited, or maintaining a facade. He does not resent or contest her allocation decisions for the same reason.
- He is genuinely worried about the Sacred Spring — not financially (most Skybound profit funds the monthly tournament, and the remainder he donates to the town), but because he loves brewing, and because his years of travel taught him to recognize the signs of deeper trouble.
- Joe knows nothing more about the Ancient Pact than the average villager. He is older, not old.
- Joe brought the idea for the tournament back from his travels, inspired by fighting rings and by the way power struggles played out while he crewed aboard a pirate ship. It is not an old village tradition — it began after he returned to find prominent villagers squabbling over who should represent Sky-Bough, joked "just fight for it," and the idea stuck.
- The village champion title is mostly ceremonial — bragging rights and free drinks at the Bent Feather. It carries no real judicial or political weight; the mayor still handles matters of actual consequence.
- Joe is not rigging the tournament and is not angling for political or economic control through it or through Skybound. The "Joe wants control" rumour has two independent, mistaken roots: outsiders overestimating what "emissary to the Heartwood" means, and pro-mayor villagers assuming his indifference to Skybound's profits masks a scheme to seize a larger share of a trade that is, despite his disinterest, a major driver of the town's economy.
- The tournament-rigging rumour specifically originated with Mossa Quickbrow, a villager of mixed Ribbet and Simiah heritage who lost cleanly but genuinely believes prejudice over their heritage cost them the match. See [[The Sky-Bough Martial Arts Tournament]].
- Joe would never voluntarily stop brewing Skybound — he loves the craft too much. Only losing the physical ability to brew (the spring failing outright) would stop him, and that would be a serious blow to Sky-Bough's economy.
- The previous proprietor left the Bent Feather to Joe out of genuine personal fondness, not obligation or scheme. The proprietor had no children of their own, and Joe had spent a great deal of time around the tavern growing up — enough that the proprietor came to see him as the right person to inherit it, whatever claim others with more formal or blood ties might have had.
- Joe left Sky-Bough out of simple restlessness and wanderlust — a sense of being stuck in the village's old ways, with nothing specific pushing him out. He spent roughly two decades away, travelling essentially everywhere; his year crewing aboard a pirate ship in the Meridian Isles (where the tournament idea came from) was just one stop among many, not the whole of his time gone.
- This is exactly why Joe and the mayor get along so genuinely well: he sees his younger self in her, chasing the same restlessness and the same frustration with Sky-Bough's old ways, before his own years away eventually wore that feeling down into something calmer. See [[The Mayor]].
