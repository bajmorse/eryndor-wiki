# Sir Testalot and the Testers

Connected to [[Full Session Notes]] in the player wiki.

Should remain somewhat ridiculous — the juxtaposition is too good — but answer enough questions that the joke religion becomes a legitimate piece of the setting.

## Current State

The Testers, followers of Sir Testalot, are a small but rapidly growing religious movement whose theology holds that mortal existence consists of a series of divine tests. Something resembling a gigantic moustache recently appeared in the clouds above Sky-Bough; the Testers interpreted it as a divine sign pointing toward the Summering Sea, and devotees have been growing moustaches, watching the skies, and arguing over its meaning.

## Rumours / What NPCs Believe

Two interpretations dominate among the Testers: that the Sea People have violated the Ancient Pact, or that the escaped sacrifice fled toward the sea and must be recovered. Scholars have found that the old Drowning cult followed an ancient faith whose teachings contain phrases eerily similar to modern Tester doctrine.
