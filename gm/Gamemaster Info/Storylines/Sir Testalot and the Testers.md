# Sir Testalot and the Testers

Connected to [[Full Session Notes]] in the player wiki.

Should remain somewhat ridiculous — the juxtaposition is too good — but answer enough questions that the joke religion becomes a legitimate piece of the setting.

## Current State

The Testers, followers of Sir Testalot, are a small but rapidly growing religious movement whose theology holds that mortal existence consists of a series of divine tests. Something resembling a gigantic moustache recently appeared in the clouds above Sky-Bough; the Testers interpreted it as a divine sign pointing toward the Summering Sea, and devotees have been growing moustaches, watching the skies, and arguing over its meaning.

## Established GM Truths

- Sir Testalot was, and still is, a real, living individual — not a myth or metaphor. He is periodically and involuntarily reshaped in body, race, class, and equipment by an unknowable higher power. He's aware something governs him and carries fragments of memory loss from each episode, but has never understood what the "higher power" is or why he was chosen.
- Scattered sightings and rumours of a shapeshifting figure muttering about being controlled by a higher power have circulated, mostly across Eldressa, since at least ~30 years before the Unification — roughly 330 years of intermittent folklore.
- The Testers as an organized religion are barely a decade old. The faith coalesced after an eccentric group of rumour-collectors found a journal written by Sir Testalot himself in the ruins of an old cabin in the Eldressa woods, and pieced together generations of scattered sightings into doctrine.
- Testers believe Sir Testalot was uniquely chosen by the higher power for testing — not that all mortals are subject to the same kind of trial.
- The moustache motif comes directly from Sir Testalot's own signature, glorious moustache. Devotees grow out facial hair believing greater moustachio-ness improves their attunement to his signs.
- The religion remains fringe and not widely practiced, but has been spreading slowly — helped along by smaller moustache-related phenomena elsewhere (moustache-shaped trees, etc.), especially across mystical Ilyndor, where the faith lands more easily than in technological Velkrand, economical Meridia, or practical Hrothan. It reached Sky-Bough roughly a year ago and has only begun gaining real local traction since the cloud incident.
- A moustache-shaped cloud genuinely appeared over Sky-Bough — this is observably true, not a hoax or mass delusion. Whether it constitutes an actual supernatural sign, or is ordinary pareidolia (the way any cloud might look like a dragon), is being left deliberately undecided — to resolve through play rather than GM fiat, the same way [[The Mayor]]'s alignment is being left open.
- Whether "pointing toward the Summering Sea" carries any literal meaning (e.g. Sir Testalot's actual current location) is likewise being left deliberately ambiguous for now.
- The two dominant Tester interpretations of the cloud — that the Sea People violated the Ancient Pact, or that the escaped sacrifice fled to the sea — are baseless speculation, believers grabbing at the most sensational regional news. Neither is true.
- There is no connection, ancestral or otherwise, between the old Drowning cult at the Sacred Spring and the Testalot religion. No scholar, Tester, or anyone else has ever suggested one — the idea has no basis in the setting.

## Rumours / What NPCs Believe

## Open Questions

- Was the moustache-shaped cloud an actual supernatural sign, or an ordinary trick of the clouds the Testers are over-reading? Deliberately left open — resolve through play, not GM fiat.
- Does "pointing toward the Summering Sea" mean anything real (e.g. is Sir Testalot actually there), or is it just as ambiguous as the cloud itself? Also deliberately left open.
