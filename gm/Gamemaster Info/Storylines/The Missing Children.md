# The Missing Children

Connected to [[Full Session Notes]] in the player wiki.

Deserves its own completely developed mystery independent of whether the players investigate it.

## Current State

Children throughout the surrounding region have begun disappearing — not just Kenku, but Simiah, Katari, Humans, and others. Every victim has been a minor, and near every disappearance the same strange symbol is found.

## Established GM Truths

## What the Party Knows

Everything under Current State. See [[Full Session Notes]].

## Rumours / What NPCs Believe

Rumours naturally point toward [[Old Man Willy]]: he collects strange relics, he lives alone, people already think he's a necromancer, and he's been acting strangely. Somebody even swears they once saw children's bones outside his house — though by all other accounts, they were chicken bones.

## Open Questions

- Exactly how many children have disappeared?
- Over what period?
- Where did each disappearance happen?
- What ages were they?
- Which ancestries?
- Is there actually a pattern beyond them being minors?
- Why children?
- What does the symbol left behind mean?
- Is the symbol deliberately being left, or is it a side effect of whatever happens?
- Who was the first child to disappear?
- Has anyone disappeared outside Sky-Bough?
- Has anyone witnessed an attempted disappearance?
- Are the children alive?
- Where are they being taken?
- Who or what is taking them?
- What does the responsible party want with them?
- Are the disappearances connected to the failed sacrifice?
- Are they connected to the ancient tomb?
- Are they connected to the Sacred Spring?
- Are they connected to the Sea People?
- Or are they an entirely separate problem that happens to be occurring simultaneously?
- What evidence makes Old Man Willy look guilty?
