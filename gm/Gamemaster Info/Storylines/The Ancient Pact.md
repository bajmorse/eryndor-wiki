# The Ancient Pact

Connected to [[Full Session Notes]] in the player wiki.

The most foundational mystery in Sky-Bough — its answers potentially inform several other threads.

## Current State

Once each year, before the growing season, Sky-Bough selects one resident by lot and sacrifices them. In exchange, the surrounding forest stays relatively safe, dangerous creatures keep their distance, and the harvest holds. Last year, for the first time in living memory, the selected sacrifice escaped before the ritual could be completed, leaving the pact unfulfilled. Months later, crop yields are down, wildlife is behaving strangely, predators are appearing closer to settled areas, and the Sacred Spring is receding.

## Established GM Truths

## What the Party Knows

Everything under Current State — the pact's terms, and that it went unfulfilled last year. See [[Full Session Notes]].

## Rumours / What NPCs Believe

Villagers increasingly connect the failing harvest, the receding Sacred Spring, and stranger wildlife behaviour to the failed sacrifice, and are beginning to ask whether the sacrifice was ever truly necessary.

## Open Questions

- Who or what was the original pact made with?
- Why was the pact created in the first place? What threatened the original inhabitants?
- What does the sacrifice actually accomplish?
- Was human sacrifice always part of the pact, or has the ritual been misunderstood or corrupted over generations?
- Why is one person chosen randomly?
- What happens to the sacrificed person? Are they simply killed, consumed by something, spiritually absorbed, transported somewhere?
- What specifically does Sky-Bough receive in return? Good harvests? Protection from monsters? Something broader?
- How directly responsible is the failed sacrifice for this year's poor harvest?
- Is the drying Sacred Spring another consequence of the failed ritual?
- What other protections are beginning to fail that the villagers haven't noticed yet?
- Who knows the most about the true history of the pact?
- Who was selected last year?
- How did they escape?
- Did someone help them?
- Where are they now?
- What do they know that the village doesn't?
- Does whatever holds the pact want them back?
- What happens when the next annual sacrifice approaches?
