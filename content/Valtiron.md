# Valtiron

Valtiron is the political heart of [[Eldressa]] and the closest thing the known world has to a global diplomatic capital. Built within the ancient crater left by the destruction of the Great Mountain during the [[The First Demon War|First Demon War]], it serves as neutral ground where the representatives of [[Eldressa]]'s sovereign powers — and increasingly of the wider world — gather to negotiate, deliberate, and attempt to resolve the disagreements that might otherwise become wars.

Valtiron governs little territory of its own. Its power is entirely diplomatic. And in the modern era, that may make it the most important city in [[Eldressa]].

---

## History

The Great Mountain — whose destruction ended the First Demon War — left behind a crater of enormous scale within what is now central [[Eldressa]]. For centuries it stood as a scar on the landscape, a reminder of catastrophe rather than a center of anything.

The transformation of the site came during the **Age of Reconnection**, when the returning [[Dragons]] helped broker the creation of a neutral political meeting ground for the fractured nations of [[Eldressa]]. The decision to build on the crater was deliberate and symbolic: peace would be constructed on the ruins of one of history's great disasters.

The [[Dragons]] envisioned Valtiron as belonging to no single kingdom — a space where representatives could meet as equals, without any one party having the home advantage. This principle has been maintained, sometimes precariously, throughout the city's existence.

The **Argent Exchange**, a powerful Eldressan mercantile consortium, played a significant role in financing the city's construction and expansion. Its continued presence in Valtiron gives the institution additional influence over the city's commercial and political life.

Over the following centuries, Valtiron grew from a meeting ground into a full city — with permanent diplomatic missions, a residential population supporting the institution, commercial districts serving visiting representatives, and the scholarly and administrative infrastructure needed to support ongoing political work.

---

## Governance

Valtiron is governed by its **Chancellor**, who presides over the [[Council of Valtiron]], manages the city's administrative affairs, and represents the institution to the wider world. The Chancellor governs [[Valtiron]] proper and the nearby settlement of **Unvis** — a modest territorial footprint, but the position's influence extends far beyond its borders.

**Chancellor Serazeth Vael (Demon, Female)** is the current Chancellor and a direct descendant of a figure involved in the Unification peace accords. She is the first [[Demons|Demon]] to hold the office, a historical milestone that was not without controversy and has nonetheless been widely recognized as successful. She is regarded by most observers as one of the most skilled diplomats in the world.

---

## The Council

The [[Council of Valtiron]] convenes within Valtiron's deliberation chambers and brings together representatives from across [[Eldressa]] and, increasingly, from [[Velkrand]], [[Meridia]], and beyond. It functions as a diplomatic forum rather than a governing body — its decisions carry moral and political weight but cannot be enforced without the cooperation of the member states that choose to honor them.

The Council is where Eldressa's sovereign powers attempt to manage their relationships with one another without resorting to war. Its record on this front is imperfect but, by historical standards, remarkable.

---

## Character

Valtiron is a city built for a purpose rather than grown organically from geography or trade. This gives it a distinctive character: orderly, institutional, and deeply conscious of its role. The permanent population consists largely of diplomats, administrators, scholars, legal experts, merchants serving visiting delegations, and the service workers supporting all of the above.

The city's architecture reflects its symbolic function. Public spaces are deliberately designed to imply equality between delegations. No nation's embassy occupies a position of obvious precedence over another's. The deliberation chambers are arranged to give no single seat a position of structural advantage.

Beneath this careful staging, Valtiron's corridors and private dining rooms are as much the real location of diplomatic work as its formal chambers. Agreements reached in Valtiron are usually shaped by conversations that happened before anyone sat down at the official table.

---

## Present Day

In 300 AU, Valtiron is at the height of its influence as an institution. The relative stability of the Modern Age has allowed diplomatic norms to deepen, relationships between member states to develop, and the Council's authority to grow through a consistent track record of managing disputes without violence.

The appointment of Chancellor Serazeth Vael represents one of the most significant political developments in the institution's history — both for what it says about Demon integration into Eldressan society and for the diplomatic skill she has demonstrated in the role.

The ongoing expansion of [[Velkrand]]'s influence, the commercial growth of [[Meridia]], and various regional tensions across [[Eldressa]] ensure that Valtiron's deliberation chambers remain active.
