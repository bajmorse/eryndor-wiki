# Humans

Humans are the most widespread people in the known world. Adaptable, diverse, and extraordinarily varied in culture and tradition, they can be found on every continent and in nearly every major city. Yet all human cultures, however distant from one another, trace a common origin to the frozen south.

---

## Origins

Humanity originated in [[Hrothan]], the vast frozen continent at the southern edge of the world. For untold ages, human tribes survived the tundra through nomadic hunting, migration, and oral tradition — the accumulated knowledge of how to endure one of the harshest environments on Eryndor.

The circumstances of humanity's creation in the age following the Shattering remain debated among scholars. Many traditions claim that displaced souls from the dying worlds of the Celestial Age reincarnated upon Eryndor and gradually became the mortal peoples known today. Human oral traditions, however, tend to focus less on cosmic origins and more on the practical reality of survival in the frozen south.

---

## The Migration

The great turning point in human history was the [[The Human Migration|migration northward]] from Hrothan into [[Eldressa]].

The trigger was the **Long Winter** — the catastrophic collapse of the great Icehorn herds that had sustained most of Hrothan's human populations for generations. As the herds failed, famine spread. Conflicts with [[Goliaths]] and [[Giants]] over dwindling resources intensified. Many leaders concluded that Hrothan could no longer support its peoples indefinitely.

The legendary figure of **Kaelor Icewalker** is credited with leading the first great migration, spending years mapping routes through glaciers and frozen passes before guiding thousands across the Frostfire Strait into the south. Whether he truly existed as a single individual or represents a composite of many leaders and journeys, virtually every human culture preserves some version of his story. Most depictions show him carrying a lantern said to contain dragonfire gifted by an ancient wyrm.

The migrants who crossed into Eldressa eventually became the ancestors of most human peoples living there today. Those who remained in Hrothan continued in the old ways — nomadic, tribal, and deeply shaped by the ancient world.

---

## Culture & Society

Human culture is among the most varied of any people on Eryndor, a direct consequence of millennia of migration, adaptation, and contact with other civilizations.

In [[Eldressa]], humans founded kingdoms, built cities, and became major participants in the continent's complex political landscape. The **Kingdom of Algade** now serves as the principal human political power in Eldressa.

In the [[Meridian Isles]], humans are the most numerically dominant people, found in roles ranging from merchant captains to adventurers to diplomats.

In [[Velkrand]], [[Hrothan]], and [[Skeldran]], humans occupy roles shaped by the specific character of each region — industrialists in Velkrand, survivalists in Hrothan, and rare explorers in Skeldran.

What unites human cultures across their enormous diversity is a general pattern of adaptability. Humans integrate into new environments, adopt and synthesize cultural practices from neighbors, and often become influential participants in societies not originally their own. Many historians view this flexibility as the primary explanation for humanity's remarkable spread.

---

## Notable Historical Figures

**Kaelor Icewalker** — The legendary leader of the First Crossing, credited with guiding the first great human migration from Hrothan into Eldressa. Most human cultures preserve some version of his story.

**Lyra Snowsong** — A beloved figure from the Long Winter who traveled between scattered tribes preserving oral histories before entire communities disappeared.

**Elyra Windfoot** — A Halfling figure, but deeply associated with the Human-Halfling trade networks; her lantern routes helped integrate arriving Human migrants into Eldressan commerce.

---

## Present Day

In 300 AU, humans are found on every continent and in virtually every major city of the known world. They are the dominant population of the [[Meridian Isles]] and [[Eldressa]]'s largest single demographic, and they maintain their ancient nomadic traditions in [[Hrothan]].

**King Edric Valen** of the Kingdom of Algade is the most influential human ruler in Eldressa. **High Storykeeper Eirik Snowborn** leads the northern tribes of Hrothan, where the old ways are still practiced by those who never left the frozen continent.

Human life expectancy is shorter than that of Elves or Dwarves, which many historians believe contributes to a cultural tendency toward urgency, ambition, and change — for better and worse.
