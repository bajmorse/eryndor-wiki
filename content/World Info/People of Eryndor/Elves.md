# Elves

Elves are among the oldest mortal civilizations in the known world, with recorded history stretching back to the earliest ages following the Shattering. Their long lifespans, deep ties to magic, and complex political traditions have made them one of the most influential peoples in [[Eldressa]] and beyond.

---

## Origins

Elven origins are intertwined with the final age of the Celestial War. Ancient myths across many cultures trace Elven ancestry to the immortalized proto-elven peoples cared for by the Angelic mage **Orelas** — the Shepherd of the Firstborn.

Orelas devoted centuries to the welfare of these early mortal people. After witnessing countless lives lost to death and extinction, he created the **Rite of Continuance** — a form of soul-binding magic that dramatically extended their lifespans. This act of compassion had unintended consequences: it disrupted the ancient cycle of souls between worlds, contributing to the chain of events that became the Celestial War.

When the Devil General **Maelthoryx** led an assault against Orelas and his charges in the **Slaughter of Lethariel**, most of the immortalized proto-elves were killed. The survivors fled deeper into the wilds, protected by the sacrifice of the Angelic warrior **Valacir**.

Many later Elven cultures mythologize the First Bestowal — the moment Orelas performed the Rite — as the divine origin of Elven longevity itself. The grief, endurance, and longevity that define so much of Elven culture can be traced, at least in myth, to this beginning.

---

## Culture & Society

Elven civilization is defined above all by time. Where [[Humans]] measure history in generations, Elves measure it in centuries. The oldest Elven rulers have lived through events that most other peoples know only from books.

This longevity creates both strength and tension. Elven institutions endure. Traditions are preserved with extraordinary fidelity. Ancient grievances, however, are also preserved with equal care.

Elven political structures tend toward aristocracy and monarchy, often with complex lineage-based hierarchies. The **Crownless War** — an ancient Elven civil conflict fought over succession and deeply opposed philosophies — permanently fractured the unified Elven political identity that once dominated early [[Eldressa]]. The divisions it created are still visible in the distinct cultures of modern Elven peoples.

The **Weeping of Brethil** — when rival houses fought within the sacred forests and the ancient trees bled silver sap — led to the Oath of Falling Leaves, forbidding warfare within the deepest groves of [[Heartwood|Brethil]]. This oath is still honored today.

## Peoples

Elves are not a single homogeneous culture. Several distinct peoples have emerged over millennia of separation, migration, and independent development.

**High Elves** are most closely associated with the ancient traditions, arcane scholarship, and the long legacy of the Elven kingdoms of Eldressa. The ruling house of the **Kingdom of Brethil** is High Elven.

**Wood Elves** developed primarily within the great forests of Eldressa and maintain the deepest spiritual connection to the land itself. Many Wood Elf traditions are intertwined with the ancient stewardship of the forests.

**Sea Elves** are found primarily in coastal communities, throughout the [[Meridian Isles]], and in the city of **Coral**. Their maritime knowledge and trade traditions have made them influential far beyond their population size.

**Drow** have developed as a distinct culture with their own traditions and philosophies, shaped by centuries of separation from other Elven peoples. Their relationship with the wider Elven world varies considerably by region.

**Primal Elves** represent the oldest surviving Elven lineages, many of whom retreated from the growing kingdoms of the First Age and maintained connections to the natural world more closely associated with the earliest Elven peoples.

## Notable Historical Figures

**Orelas — The Shepherd of the Firstborn** — The Angelic mage whose Rite of Continuance is said to be the origin of Elven longevity. Killed during the Slaughter of Lethariel.

**Queen Althiriel Vaelor** — The Elven queen whose death triggered the Crownless War and permanently fractured early Elven unity.

**Seredain the White** — One of the last Dragons openly known to guide Elven civilization, whose disappearance marked the end of the Age of Dragon Stewardship.

## Present Day

Elven civilization in 300 AU is politically diverse and culturally rich, distributed across [[Eldressa]], the [[Meridian Isles]], and scattered communities elsewhere.

The **Kingdom of Brethil**, ruled by **Queen Aeloria Starbloom**, is one of the oldest continuously operating governments in Eryndor. Queen Starbloom is among the oldest political leaders in the world, and her influence extends well beyond the borders of Brethil into Eldressan politics and scholarship.

The **Woods of Brethil** themselves remain among the most sacred locations in Elven culture — protected by the Oath of Falling Leaves and maintained through centuries of careful stewardship.

**Governor Neris Tidecaller** leads the governing assembly of **Coral**, one of Eldressa's most prosperous and politically independent coastal cities.

The long lifespan of Elves means that many individuals alive today have living memory of the Second Demon War and its aftermath — a perspective that shapes how they view the current era of peace differently than shorter-lived peoples might.
