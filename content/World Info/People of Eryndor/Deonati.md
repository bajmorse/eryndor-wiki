# Deonati

The Deonati are a people of extraordinary diversity, united by a common spiritual heritage and the ability to take on animal forms or traits. Shapeshifters descended from ancient animal spirits shaped by the primordial forces of the Beastlands, they were once concentrated in [[Eldressa]] before displacement and the long journey of the [[The Human Migration|Path of Falling Leaves]] brought them to [[Ilyndor]], now their oldest and most sacred homeland. Their society is still recovering from centuries of persecution and diaspora.

---

## Origins

Deonati origins are deeply tied to the age following the Shattering, when the Beastlands sent divine emissaries into the recovering world. Through these influences, the first Deonati emerged — mortal beings imbued with the spiritual essence of animals, capable of shifting between forms and carrying within them a connection to the primal forces of nature.

The most revered figure in Deonati tradition from this earliest age is **Aiyana Spiritwalker — Mother of the First Deonati**. She is said to have been among the first to recognize the bond between her people and the [[Heartwood|Woodland Guardians]] of [[Ilyndor]], traveling the continent to learn from each Guardian and carry their teachings between distant tribes. She is remembered not as a ruler but as a teacher and unifier.

---

## Forms and Lineages

The Deonati are not a single form. Different lineages carry different animal heritages, and these distinctions have shaped culture, geography, and tradition across the people's long history.

**Kitsune** — Fox-lineage Deonati, associated with wit, storytelling, and travel. The legendary **Sayuri Nine-Trails** — the Laughing Pathfinder — was a Kitsune wanderer credited with carrying songs, stories, and customs between isolated communities across [[Ilyndor]], helping form a shared Ilyndoran cultural identity before any formal political structures existed.

**Tanuki** — Known for warmth, commerce, and social intelligence. The Elder Speaker **Sayume Riverstep** and the Jade Current Company trader **Momo Bramblepouch** are both Tanuki Deonati of considerable influence.

**Katari** — A warrior-guardian lineage whose traditions are centered around the protection of the [[Heartwood]]. The **Katari of the Heartwood Tribes** serve as the primary guardians of Ilyndor's sacred center. Their duty is treated as a solemn, inherited obligation tracing back to the legendary **Kael Thornwarden — The First Guardian**.

**Orcinus** — A seafaring lineage deeply tied to deep-water sailing. **Makoa Deepcurrent**, one of the three Pirate Lords of Nadris, is an Orcinus Deonati and one of the most respected sailors alive.

Other lineages are found throughout the world, carrying different animal heritages and cultural traditions shaped by the regions they have inhabited.

## History

### Early Age and the Path of Falling Leaves

The earliest Deonati communities developed in [[Ilyndor]], shaped by the **Woodland Guardians** who presided over the Heartwood. The **Great Gathering of Antlers** — one of the earliest continent-wide events in Ilyndoran memory — brought together dozens of Deonati, Katari, Faerie, Faun, and Galapa communities to establish peaceful coexistence. Unlike treaties of other continents, the Gathering was never written down; each people carried their part through song, dance, and oral tradition.

During the early First Age, several Deonati clans undertook the **Path of Falling Leaves** — a historic migration westward across the sea into [[Eldressa]], led by the explorer **Naru Windtail — The Pathfinder**. The reasons for this journey are debated; some traditions claim the Woodland Guardians foresaw coming changes; others say the Deonati simply believed new experiences strengthened the soul. Whatever the cause, the migration permanently connected the histories of Ilyndor and Eldressa, and many Deonati found in Eldressa today trace their ancestry to this crossing.

### Persecution and Displacement

Deonati history in [[Eldressa]] was marked by extended periods of persecution and displacement as human and Elven kingdoms expanded. The details of this history are painful and complex, and much of it is preserved within Deonati oral tradition rather than the written records of the kingdoms that carried it out.

Over centuries, many Deonati communities in Eldressa were pushed out, marginalized, or destroyed. Those who could, eventually made their way back toward [[Ilyndor]] — the continent that had always been their oldest homeland.

## Culture & Society

Deonati culture places immense value on oral tradition, community bonds, ancestral memory, and the relationship between mortal life and the natural world. Shapeshifting is not merely a physical ability — it is understood as a spiritual expression of the connection between mortal beings and the animal spirits they descend from.

Governance is local rather than hierarchical. Most Deonati communities are led by councils of elders, with authority coming from wisdom, demonstrated judgment, and respect within the community rather than hereditary title. Decisions that affect the wider community are made through discussion and consensus.

The traditions surrounding the **Heartwood** are central to spiritual life for most Deonati, even those who have never visited [[Ilyndor]]. The Woodland Guardians are regarded as ancestral progenitors rather than gods — powerful, ancient, deeply wise, but mortal.

---

## Present Day

In 300 AU, Deonati communities are found primarily in [[Ilyndor]], where they represent the most organized settled population on the continent. Modern Deonati society is engaged in an ongoing process of cultural rebuilding following the long history of persecution and displacement.

**Elder Speaker Sayume Riverstep**, a Tanuki Deonati, is one of the most respected Deonati leaders alive and serves as a diplomat connecting her people with the wider world.

In [[Ilyndor]], Deonati settlements govern themselves through local councils and elected elders. The **Jade Current Company**, which facilitates trade between Ilyndor and [[Meridia]], employs several prominent Deonati figures and is seen as an important bridge between Ilyndoran traditions and the global economy.

Deonati are also found in [[Meridia]], across [[Eldressa]], and in the [[Meridian Isles]] — particularly in Nadris, where **Makoa Deepcurrent** commands one of the most respected deep-water fleets in the world.
