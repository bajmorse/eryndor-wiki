# Timeline

A chronological overview of the major eras and events of Eryndor's history, from the Celestial Age to the present day.

---

## Before the Mortal World

### The Divine War
*Era unknown — predates the Mortal Plane*

The gods fought a catastrophic conflict before the creation of the mortal world. The victors became the **New Gods** and claimed the Hallows Above. The defeated became the **Fallen Gods** and were banished into the Circles Below. As part of the peace that followed, the Mortal Plane was created as a cosmic balance — and the Angels, Devils, and Dragons were placed within it as balancing forces.

---

## The Celestial Age
*Era unknown*

The three immortal powers — [[Angels|Angels]], [[Demons|Devils]], and [[Dragons]] — governed existence across the three Prime Worlds: Eryndor, Gontkhael, and Nythorak. [[Dragons]] maintained cosmic balance between the creative principle of the Angels and the transformative principle of the Devils. The mortal races did not yet exist.

---

## The Celestial War
*Era unknown — ends with the Shattering*

- **The First Bestowal** — The Angelic mage Orelas performs the Rite of Continuance, extending the lifespans of the proto-elven peoples. This unknowingly disrupts the cycle of souls between worlds.
- **The Silence of Dragons** — As the imbalance worsens, the Dragons repeatedly fail to intervene.
- **The Slaughter of Lethariel** — Devil General Maelthoryx massacres the immortalized proto-elven civilization. Orelas is killed. The Angelic warrior Valacir dies defending survivors. The Celestial War begins in earnest.
- **The Sundering Debate** — The last conclave between Angels, Devils, and Dragons ends without agreement.
- **The Battle of Hollow Heaven** — Celestial combat begins damaging reality itself.
- **The Burning of Gontkhael** — Gontkhael collapses beneath the strain of the conflict.
- **The Final Invocation / The Shattering** — The Angels harness the Soulstone to end the war. The Soulstone shatters. Gontkhael breaks apart. The Devils and Nythorak are banished into the Void. The surviving Angels are permanently transformed into [[Demons]].

---

## The Age of Rebirth
*Era unknown — post-Shattering*

Eryndor survives the Shattering largely intact, though permanently scarred. Life slowly returns. The Beastlands send divine emissaries into the recovering world. The Colossi, Woodland Guardians, Forge Titans, and Leviathans serve as First Stewards across the continents.

---

## Rise of the Mortal Races
*Era unknown*

The great mortal civilizations emerge across Eryndor. Displaced souls from the dying worlds of Gontkhael and Nythorak gradually become the mortal peoples of the modern age. [[Elves]], [[Dwarves]], [[Humans]], [[Halflings]], [[Deonati]], and others develop their earliest civilizations.

---

## Age of Dragon Stewardship
*Era unknown*

[[Dragons]], burdened by guilt over the Silence of Dragons, take responsibility for guiding the emerging mortal races. In [[Eldressa]], they serve as teachers, mediators, and guardians. Early Elven kingdoms, Dwarven holds, and Human settlements develop beneath their guidance.

**Key events:**
- The Accord of Sky and Stone between the early Dwarven clans and the Dragon steward Ithrymath
- The War of Ash and Iron between early Elven kingdoms and Dwarven mountain holds
- The Crownless War fractures early Elven unity
- The Weeping of Brethil; the Oath of Falling Leaves is sworn
- The Path of Falling Leaves — Deonati clans migrate from [[Ilyndor]] to [[Eldressa]]
- The Great Gathering of Antlers in Ilyndor
- Seredain the White — The Last Dragon Steward — disappears

---

## The Leviathan War
*Era unknown — predates the First Demon War*

The great primordial Leviathans of [[Skeldran]] turn upon one another. The continent is shattered. The ancient peoples of Skeldran — the Selkari, the Tidemarked, and the Corvani — lose their civilizations. All nine Leviathans perish. The Silence of the Leviathans marks the end of Skeldran's First Age.

---

## The First Demon War
*Era: under revision*

A major conflict tied to the arrival of [[Demons|Demonkind]] in Eryndor. The Great Mountain is destroyed during this conflict, devastating [[Velkrand]] through the **Ashfall Catastrophe** — volcanic eruptions in the north, tsunamis on the southern coast. The Accord of Twin Hammers ends centuries of Goblin-Gnome conflict in the aftermath. The Clank are eventually created during the reconstruction that follows.

---

## Age of Reconstruction
*Era unknown*

Civilizations rebuild. Trade expands. The great cities of the modern world begin to take shape. The Goblin-Gnome reconstruction of Velkrand lays the industrial foundations of that continent.

---

## The Second Demon War
*Era: under revision*

A second major conflict that reshaped the modern political landscape. It ended with the **Unification** — the sealing of the Void Rift and the establishment of peace between the peoples of Eryndor and the [[Demons]].

---

## The Modern Age
*Year 0 AU — Present (~300 AU)*

Modern calendars measure time from the Unification:
- **BU** — Before Unification
- **AU** — After Unification

### Reconstruction Era (0–80 AU)
Rebuilding of Eldressa's war-devastated regions. Many Demons choose to remain in Eryndor. Cities are restored. The Velkrand Consolidation begins, leading eventually to political unification under the Twin Thrones. The Bank of Unification is founded in [[Rivara]].

### Age of Reconnection (80–170 AU)
[[Dragons]] return to the world. Dragon Riders emerge as a global communication and travel network. [[Valtiron]] is founded in the crater of the destroyed Great Mountain, becoming the political heart of [[Eldressa]]. The world begins to connect across continental distances for the first time.

### Age of Discovery (170–250 AU)
[[Meridia]] grows from a modest port into the world's largest and most influential city as trade routes expand. The Bank of Unification relocates to Meridia. Skeldran is increasingly explored. Modern international institutions take shape.

### Modern Era (250 AU–Present)
Relative stability and unprecedented global interconnectedness. Trade flourishes between continents. The current year is approximately **300 AU**.
