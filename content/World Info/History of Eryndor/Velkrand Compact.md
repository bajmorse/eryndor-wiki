# Velkrand Compact

The Velkrand Compact is the growing political and economic alliance driving the unification of [[Velkrand]]'s historically independent powers. What began as a series of post-war commercial agreements between the surviving Goblin and Gnomish peoples has evolved, over three centuries, into the most significant political project underway in the modern world: the potential creation of the first truly unified continent.

---

## Origins

The foundation of the Compact traces back to the **Accord of Twin Hammers** — the treaty negotiated in the ruins of the Ashfall Catastrophe by the last surviving leaders of the Goblin Forge-Clans and the Gnomish League. The accord ended centuries of Goblin-Gnome rivalry and established the principle of shared civilization on [[Velkrand]].

The **Gathering of Three Paths** — a rare peaceful assembly that followed, bringing Goblin, Gnome, and Firbolg representatives together — built on the Accord to establish that Velkrand's future would be built through cooperation rather than conquest.

These agreements were formal and limited at first: economic partnerships, shared infrastructure, mutual defense arrangements. Over time, as the economic logic of cooperation became undeniable and cross-community relationships deepened, the agreements evolved into something more like a genuine political union.

The **Velkrand Consolidation** — the broad period of political integration that culminated in the Twin Thrones of Grimwald — is now treated by historians as the moment the Compact ceased to be merely a commercial arrangement and became the governing framework of a continent.

---

## Structure

The Compact is embodied today in the **Twin Thrones of Grimwald** — a dual monarchy jointly occupied by one Goblin and one Gnome ruler, symbolizing the partnership that ended centuries of rivalry and built modern Velkrand.

**King Ragnik Embercrown (Goblin, Male)** represents the northern Goblin city-states and serves as co-ruler of Velkrand.

**Queen Elira Cogsworth (Gnome, Female)** represents the southern Gnomish cities and serves as co-ruler of Velkrand.

The dual throne is not merely symbolic. The requirement for joint agreement between the Goblin and Gnomish leadership on major decisions builds structural consensus into the governance of the continent, preventing either community from dominating the other. Critics argue this creates gridlock; supporters argue it ensures neither half of the partnership feels subordinated.

The **Clank** — who emerged as a people during the reconstruction era — hold a distinctive place within the Compact's political structure, wielding administrative and economic influence disproportionate to what a straightforwardly political accounting might suggest.

The **Firbolg** communities of Velkrand's central forests maintain their own relationship with the Compact through formal forest protection agreements and representation in commercial negotiations.

---

## Economic Power

The Compact's economic institutions are among its most significant features.

The **Iron Ledger Consortium** — the largest industrial trading organization in the world — was founded jointly by Goblin engineers, Gnomish artificers, and Clank administrators and coordinates the production, transportation, and export of much of Velkrand's industrial output. Its influence extends into nearly every major city of the known world. **Professor Tinkwick Coppergear** serves as its Chief Executive Artificer; **Unit-7A "Ledger"** (Clank) serves as its Administrative Coordinator.

The **Embercrown Mercantile League** — originally formed by northern Goblin city-states seeking independence from southern Gnomish economic influence — controls many mining operations, foundries, and northern trade routes. Its relationship with the Iron Ledger Consortium is competitive but generally commercial rather than political.

---

## The Compact and the Wider World

From the perspective of the other major powers — particularly [[Eldressa]] and [[Meridia]] — the Velkrand Compact is watched with a mixture of admiration and caution.

Velkrand's industrial output already underpins the global economy. The Unified Crown is minted primarily in Velkrand. The machinery, rail technology, and refined metals that every other continent depends on come from Velkrand's forges. A fully unified Velkrand with a single political voice would represent a significant shift in the global balance of power.

The [[Council of Valtiron]] maintains active diplomatic engagement with the Twin Thrones. [[Rivara]]'s merchant houses negotiate carefully with Velkrand's industrial interests. [[Meridia]] watches the Compact's expansion with the pragmatic attention of a city whose prosperity depends on trade flowing smoothly between all parties.

---

## Present Day

In 300 AU, the Velkrand Compact represents the most advanced political unification project in the known world. The Twin Thrones govern the continent, though regional identities and the autonomy of individual cities — particularly **Steamreach**, led by **Governor Vexa Coalvein** — remain significant political realities beneath the unified framework.

Many political observers believe Velkrand will be the first truly unified continent in the modern age. Whether that unification, when it comes, will shift the global balance of power — and how the other major players will respond — is one of the defining open questions of the modern era.
