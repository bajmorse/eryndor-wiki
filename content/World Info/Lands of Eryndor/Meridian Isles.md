# Meridian Isles

![[meridian-isles.png|900]]

## Location

The Meridian Isles — also known as the Freewaters — occupy the strategic center of the known world, positioned at the crossroads of maritime trade routes connecting [[Eldressa]], [[Velkrand]], [[Ilyndor]], [[Hrothan]], and [[Skeldran]]. The archipelago consists of hundreds of islands of varying size, the vast majority of which remain wild, sparsely settled, or entirely unexplored. The two great cities of the Isles — [[Meridia]] and Nadris — sit on the largest and most accessible islands, separated by open water and sharply contrasting philosophies.

---

## Description

The Meridian Isles are defined by movement. Ships from every continent pass through the Freewaters. Merchants, diplomats, explorers, adventurers, smugglers, scholars, privateers, and refugees all find their way here eventually, because every major sea lane in the known world touches these islands at some point.

The result is a place of extraordinary cultural density and contradictions. [[Meridia]] presents itself as the diplomatic and economic capital of the world — neutral, cosmopolitan, and governed by practical commerce. Nadris rejects Meridia's authority entirely, operating by its own code and fiercely protecting freedom of trade outside any institution's oversight. Most of the archipelago ignores both cities entirely, remaining as wild and ungoverned as it was in the First Age.

No single power controls the Freewaters. That may be the most important political fact in the world.

---

## Geography

**[[Meridia]]** sits on the largest of the central islands, its harbor deep and its location commanding. The city has grown rapidly over the past two centuries into the largest urban settlement in the known world, sprawling across its island in layers of docks, markets, embassies, financial institutions, and residential districts representing every people and culture of Eryndor.

**Nadris** sits on a neighboring island within sight of Meridia's harbor lights — close enough to be in constant commercial dialogue, far enough to maintain its independence. The city is built into dramatic coastal terrain with deep anchorages capable of sheltering large fleets. It operates as a free port under the governance of three Pirate Lords and maintains a strict internal code of conduct despite — or because of — its reputation.

**The outer islands** number in the hundreds and vary enormously. Some are inhabited by small fishing communities or isolated settlements. Others have been claimed by merchant interests for agricultural use. Many are densely forested with no permanent human presence at all. A handful are known to be dangerous in ways that sailors decline to explain in detail.

**The northern Meridian Protectorates** are a cluster of tropical islands along the northern edge of the archipelago, settled by Meridian commercial interests and home to resort developments, trading posts, and plantation operations. Several of these islands also serve as waypoints on the trade routes to [[Ilyndor]].

## Races / Biology

The Meridian Isles support no native race in the way that [[Hrothan]] belongs to humanity or [[Ilyndor]] to the Deonati. They belong to whoever arrives and stays.

**Humans** are the most numerically dominant people throughout the islands, found in every settlement from Meridia's merchant quarter to the smallest outer island fishing village.

**Halflings** are heavily represented in the merchant and financial communities of Meridia, where their commercial acumen and social intelligence make them natural brokers.

**Sea Elves** maintain communities throughout the archipelago, particularly on smaller and more remote islands. Their maritime knowledge is unrivaled and many serve in roles that require navigation through the more treacherous outer waters.

**Gnomes** arrived through trade connections with [[Velkrand]] and are well established in Meridia's commercial and financial districts.

**Deonati** of various ancestral lines are found throughout the islands, particularly in communities with strong connections to [[Ilyndor]].

**Demons** have established embassies and diplomatic presences in Meridia since the peace accords, and a small permanent Demon community has taken root in the city.

**Orcinus Deonati** — a seafaring Deonati lineage — are especially prominent in Nadris, where their deep-water sailing expertise gives them significant status.

The outer islands shelter ecosystems of remarkable variety. Warm northern waters support tropical marine life; deeper channels between islands are known to harbor large and occasionally dangerous sea creatures. Dragon riders regularly transit the archipelago, and it is considered exceptionally poor practice to threaten a Dragon or its rider within the Freewaters.

## History

For most of recorded history, the Meridian Isles were irrelevant.

The islands possessed no known First Steward during the earliest ages. For much of the First Age they remained wild and largely uninhabited, their treacherous waters and remote location preventing large-scale settlement. The great civilizations of [[Eldressa]] and the industrial peoples of [[Velkrand]] had little reason to venture there, and limited means to do so safely.

This began to change slowly during the Age of Dragon Stewardship as seafaring technology improved. Small settlements appeared on the most accessible islands. Trade routes began threading through the outer archipelago. But the islands remained peripheral for centuries more.

The decisive transformation came during the **Age of Reconnection**, when the return of the Dragons made travel between continents dramatically easier and faster than it had ever been before. Dragon riders could cross oceans in days. Maritime routes that had once required months of dangerous navigation became well-traveled highways.

The Meridian Isles, positioned at the intersection of these expanding routes, became the natural meeting point of the world almost overnight. Merchants who once traded with distant partners through intermediaries could now meet directly in a neutral location. Diplomats who once sent messages that took months to arrive could convene in person.

**Meridia** grew from a modest port into one of the largest cities in the world within the space of a few generations. The **Age of Discovery** saw the city consolidate its position as the world's commercial and diplomatic hub. The **[[Bank of Unification]]** relocated its headquarters there from [[Rivara|Rivara]] as global trade expanded. International guilds, trade houses, and diplomatic missions established permanent presences. The city became, effectively, the neutral ground where the world's business was conducted.

**Nadris** grew in a different direction — as a counterweight to Meridia's institutional power, a haven for those who preferred commerce without oversight, and eventually as the home of its own political tradition centered around the three Pirate Lords.

## Present Day

The Meridian Isles in 300 AU are the commercial and diplomatic center of the known world.

**[[Meridia]]** is governed by **Admiral Celeste Fairwind**, a former sailor and merchant captain widely respected for pragmatic leadership and genuine commitment to the city's neutrality. Much of the city's daily administration is managed by its **Merchant Council**, currently led by **Mira Goodbarrel**. Meridia hosts the headquarters of the [[Bank of Unification]], embassies of every major power including the Dragon Riders, and dozens of international guilds and trade houses.

**Nadris** operates under a three-way governance shared among its Pirate Lords: **Captain Grumm Bronzetide**, known for his outsized personality and fierce ambition; **Makoa Deepcurrent**, master of the deep-water fleets and one of the most respected sailors alive; and **Nine-Fingers Mara Voss**, whose influence as a smuggler, negotiator, and political strategist extends far beyond the city itself. Nadris and Meridia maintain an uneasy but economically inseparable relationship — officially rivals, practically partners.

The **outer islands** continue to attract explorers, settlers, and entrepreneurs. Several expeditions operating out of Meridia use the outer archipelago as a staging ground for further exploration, and new discoveries in the more remote islands are reported regularly.

The Freewaters remain ungoverned as a whole. No power has yet found the means — or the will — to change that.
