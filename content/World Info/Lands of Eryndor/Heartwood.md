# Heartwood

The Heartwood is the sacred center of [[Ilyndor]] — the ancient forest at the continent's interior heart from which the spiritual and cultural identity of the continent radiates outward. It is the home of the Woodland Guardians, the ancestral spiritual ground of the [[Deonati]], the protected domain of the Katari, and the site of some of the most significant events in Ilyndoran history.

It is not a nation, does not have a government, and holds no formal political authority. Its authority is moral, spiritual, and ancient, and in [[Ilyndor]] that is often more binding than any law.

---

## Description

The Heartwood is old in a way that makes even [[Eldressa]]'s ancient forests seem recent. The trees at its deepest core are vast — larger than most structures mortals build, older than most kingdoms — and the ecosystem they sustain is unlike anything found elsewhere in the world. Species of plant and animal found nowhere else thrive in its shadows. The air itself feels different to those who travel within it, as though something beneath the canopy is paying attention.

The deep Heartwood is not freely traversed. Many portions are considered sacred and are kept deliberately inaccessible to those without the knowledge and relationships necessary to navigate them. The Katari who serve as its guardians do not enforce this through walls or armies but through presence, tradition, and the understanding — shared across virtually every culture in [[Ilyndor]] — that the Heartwood's sanctity is not a political rule but a fundamental truth.

---

## The Woodland Guardians

The Woodland Guardians are the most significant beings associated with the Heartwood. They are immense primordial creatures — neither beasts nor gods — who embody aspects of the natural world: forests, rivers, seasons, growth, decay, renewal. Though mortal, their lifespans are so vast that entire civilizations have risen and fallen within the life of a single Guardian.

The peoples of [[Ilyndor]] have often revered them as deities. The [[Deonati]] regard them differently — as ancestral progenitors and spiritual forebears, immensely wise and powerful beings who share descent from the same primal forces that shaped the first Deonati. This distinction — between worship and kinship — is important to Deonati tradition.

Most Woodland Guardians from the earliest ages have vanished, withdrawn, or perished. A few survive into the present, their continued existence a living connection to the world's deep past.

**Aruun Rootfather** is the oldest and most respected of the surviving Guardians and serves as the primary voice among their number. He is treated with enormous deference by virtually every people of [[Ilyndor]] and his counsel is sought — though not always followed — by leaders across the continent.

The Covenant of Moon and Root, an ancient agreement between the Faerie Courts and the Woodland Guardians, established sacred boundaries protecting portions of the Heartwood from exploitation and remains nominally in effect.

---

## The Katari

The Katari are the primary guardians of the Heartwood — warrior-guardians whose duty to protect the sacred forests is treated as a solemn, inherited obligation. Their tradition traces back to the legendary figure of **Kael Thornwarden — The First Guardian**, who spent decades walking the Heartwood's boundaries, learning every path and hidden glade, and who served as mediator and protector when conflicts arose between expanding settlements and sacred land.

Modern Katari are led by **Chief Warden Takoro Leafstride**, a respected diplomat and defender of the Heartwood who also serves as a principal representative of the **Jade Current Company** — demonstrating the dual role many Heartwood communities now play between ancient tradition and the demands of global trade.

---

## Historical Events

**The Great Gathering of Antlers** — One of the earliest continent-wide events in [[Ilyndor]]ian history, held beneath the ancient boughs of the Heartwood. Representatives of dozens of Deonati, Katari, Faerie, Faun, and Galapa communities gathered to establish peaceful coexistence. The Gathering's agreements were never written down; each people carried their portion through song, dance, and oral tradition.

**The Covenant of Moon and Root** — The ancient agreement between the Faerie Courts and the Woodland Guardians establishing sacred protections for the Heartwood's deepest regions.

**The Silent Bloom** — A mysterious event from the ancient past in which every sacred tree within the Heartwood flowered simultaneously. The Woodland Guardians emerged openly. Predators hunted beside prey. Ancient rivalries ceased. The event has never been explained. The phrase *"May you see the Silent Bloom"* remains a blessing throughout much of [[Ilyndor]].

**The Blackroot Blight** — A mysterious corruption that spread through portions of the Heartwood in the past, turning ancient forests black and twisting plant life into hostile forms. Several Woodland Guardians perished attempting to contain it. Though considered contained, small pockets of Blackroot still occasionally appear. Many believe the corruption never truly ended.

**Sereth the Thorn King** — A Katari guardian who became convinced that mortals themselves were the greatest threat to nature, turned to increasingly extreme methods, destroyed several settlements, and had to be stopped by a coalition of Guardians and Katari. He stands as one of the Heartwood's great cautionary figures.

## Present Day

In 300 AU, the Heartwood remains the spiritual center of [[Ilyndor]] and the beating heart of the continent's cultural identity. The Woodland Guardians — led by Aruun Rootfather — continue to serve as the closest thing the continent has to shared authority, their counsel sought even by communities that govern themselves independently.

The **Jade Current Company's** growing trade relationship between [[Ilyndor]] and [[Meridia]] has brought new commercial activity to the continent's northern reaches, raising ongoing questions about how much outside commerce the Heartwood's communities should permit and on what terms. The Katari's involvement in the Jade Current Company represents one attempt to answer that question: guide the trade rather than resist it.

The Heartwood itself remains, as it has always been, the place where the world remembers what it was before civilization began.
