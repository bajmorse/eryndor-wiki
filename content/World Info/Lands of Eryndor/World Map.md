# World Map

![[eryndor.png|900]]

The known world of Eryndor consists of six major continents and the archipelago of the [[Meridian Isles]], connected by maritime trade routes and, in the modern era, by [[Dragons|Dragon]] riders.

The world's climate is inverted from what many might expect: the north is warmer and more hospitable, while the south grows progressively colder and harsher. This shapes everything from agriculture and settlement patterns to migration routes and the character of the peoples who inhabit each region.

## Continents

- [[Eldressa]] — The historical heart of civilization, most populous and politically influential.
- [[Velkrand]] — The industrial continent of forge-cities, engineers, and maritime republics.
- [[Ilyndor]] — The great primal wilderness, home of the Deonati and the Heartwood.
- [[Meridian Isles]] — The maritime crossroads of the world.
- [[Skeldran]] — A shattered northern continent, ancient ruin of the First Age.
- [[Hrothan]] — The frozen southern homeland of humanity.
