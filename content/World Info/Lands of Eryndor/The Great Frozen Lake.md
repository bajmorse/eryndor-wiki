# The Great Frozen Lake

**Lake Varglas** — known throughout [[Hrothan]] and beyond as the Great Frozen Lake — is the largest freshwater lake in the known world and one of the most sacred locations in northern culture. It lies at the heart of the **Pale Expanse**, [[Hrothan]]'s vast tundra interior, and serves simultaneously as a trade route, a gathering place, a subject of myth, and the site of a mystery that no living being has resolved.

---

## Description

For most of the year, Lake Varglas is ice. Not a surface of ice over water, but a vast expanse of ice that appears to stretch beyond the horizon in every direction — a plain as navigable as land, as featureless as sky, as unforgiving as the continent that surrounds it.

During the brief warmer months, the ice retreats partially toward the lake's center, revealing cold dark water and the massive scale of the basin itself. The lake is deep — how deep, no reliable measurement has been made. The water is extraordinarily clear when not covered.

Many northern traditions claim that on particularly clear nights, when the ice is at its most transparent, it becomes possible to see visions of the past beneath its surface. Scholars debate this endlessly. Those who have traveled the lake tend to speak about it quietly rather than dismissively.

---

## Origins

The most widely shared origin myth across [[Hrothan]]'s cultures holds that Lake Varglas formed when a dying **Colossus** lay down upon the tundra and slowly sank into the earth over countless ages, leaving behind a basin so immense that it filled with meltwater.

Whether this is literally true — whether the bones of that Colossus lie somewhere in the lake's depths — is among the questions that the second great mystery of Varglas addresses.

---

## The Sleeping Beneath

Something enormous is believed to lie dormant beneath Lake Varglas.

This is not merely folklore. The belief spans every major cultural tradition in [[Hrothan]] — Human tribes, Goliath clans, and Giant lineages all preserve versions of it, though descriptions of what lies beneath vary dramatically.

Some traditions speak of an ancient Colossus that never fully sank — still alive, still present in some diminished form beneath the ice. Others claim it is a **Leviathan** that somehow reached the frozen north from [[Skeldran]], resting beyond its range in waters it can no longer leave. Others suggest something without a name from before the mortal age.

Every few generations, tremors ripple across Lake Varglas without seismic explanation — no earthquakes in the surrounding region, no volcanic activity, no identifiable cause. The tremors have been recorded and observed across multiple centuries.

Most northerners agree on only one thing regarding whatever lies beneath: it should be left alone.

---

## Historical Significance

Lake Varglas has been the site of some of the most significant moments in Human history.

**The Gathering of a Thousand Fires** — one of the largest peaceful assemblies in recorded Hrothan history — was held on the shores and frozen surface of Varglas. For seven nights, representatives of Human tribes, Goliath clans, and Giant lineages gathered to settle feuds, arrange marriages, establish trade routes, and exchange songs. Many historians regard it as the moment when [[Hrothan]]'s peoples briefly saw themselves as fellow survivors of the same world.

The lake's shores served as the meeting point for communities spread across the vast Pale Expanse, and the tradition of using the frozen surface as a winter road made it a practical as well as symbolic center of northern life for thousands of years.

---

## Eira Icebound

The most celebrated figure associated with Lake Varglas is **Eira Icebound — The Keeper of Reflections**, one of the most beloved storytellers in [[Hrothan]]'s history.

Eira spent much of her life traveling the shores of Varglas, collecting stories from distant tribes. She believed that every story carried a fragment of truth and devoted herself to preserving them before they could be forgotten. Many of the legends surrounding the Colossi, the Giants, the Long Winter, and the Human Migration survive today because she wrote them down.

Northern bards often say that every storyteller learns from Eira, whether they know it or not.

---

## Present Day

In 300 AU, Lake Varglas remains a central landmark of [[Hrothan]] — a trade route in winter, a gathering place during the brief warmer months, and an ongoing source of myth, reverence, and unease.

Human tribes under the **High Storykeeper Eirik Snowborn** continue to traverse its frozen surface during migration and use its shores as traditional meeting grounds. The name Snowborn carries deliberate echo of the old stories — a reminder of continuity with the world before the Migration took most of humanity north.

The question of what lies beneath the lake is periodically taken up by scholarly expeditions from [[Meridia]] and [[Eldressa]], most of which conclude that the logistical difficulty of operating on Lake Varglas makes investigation impractical. None of them have been willing to fully dismiss the tremors. None of them have an explanation for the tremors either.

The northerners who actually live beside the lake tend to observe these expeditions with something between amusement and concern. They have been watching the ice for generations. They are not surprised that outsiders cannot explain what they see there.
