# Ilyndor

![[ilyndor.png|900]]

## Location

Ilyndor is the great wilderness continent of the known world. Its geography is rugged and varied — ancient forests, highland valleys, river networks, western jungles, and a jagged southern mountain range — drawing comparison to landscapes of extraordinary natural diversity. The northern coast is warm and tropical, while the southern regions grow progressively more rugged and isolated. The sacred Heartwood sits at the continent's interior heart. Several tropical islands along the northern coast have been settled by interests from [[Meridian Isles|Meridia]].

---

## Description

Ilyndor is not wild in the sense of being undeveloped. It is wild in the sense of being alive — shaped by forces older than civilization, tended by beings of immense spiritual power, and inhabited by peoples who built their cultures around knowledge of the land rather than dominion over it.

Where [[Eldressa]] measures its greatness by the cities it has built and the dynasties it has outlasted, Ilyndor measures its greatness by the forests still standing, the rivers still running clean, and the traditions still remembered. Its peoples developed immense sophistication in oral history, artistry, ecological stewardship, natural magic, and spiritual philosophy — none of which required empire to sustain.

The continent has no centralized government and has never had one. That is not an absence. It is a choice, renewed across generations.

## Geography

**The Heartwood** is the sacred center of Ilyndor and the spiritual heart of the continent. Ancient beyond reckoning, the deep forest at Ilyndor's core is the home of the Woodland Guardians and the site of the continent's most significant cultural and spiritual events. Most peoples of Ilyndor orient their traditions in some relation to the Heartwood, whether they live near it or not.

**The ancient forests** cover vast portions of the continent. These are old-growth woodlands that have existed since before the first mortal civilizations and shelter ecosystems of extraordinary complexity. In some regions the trees are so large and old they are considered sacred in their own right.

**The western jungles** are dense, warm, and poorly understood even by many Ilyndorans. A mysterious event known as the Night of Falling Stars permanently altered several regions of the western jungle, creating new rivers, revealing ancient ruins, and introducing previously unknown species. The Faerie Courts decline to explain what happened.

**The highlands and mountain valleys** of the interior are home to many of the continent's traditional communities, offering natural defensibility and access to both forest resources and open sky.

**The rugged southern mountains** form the continent's southern boundary — a harsh, relatively sparsely inhabited range that grows progressively more remote.

**The northern coast** is warm and tropical, where several islands have been settled as Meridian Protectorates in recent centuries.

## Races / Biology

**Deonati** are the most culturally prominent people on the continent and consider Ilyndor their oldest and most sacred homeland. Shapeshifters with deep spiritual ties to the natural world, the Deonati trace their ancestry to the ancient influence of the Woodland Guardians and the primal forces of nature. Their communities are organized around local councils and elected elders, and their traditions vary widely between clans, regions, and ancestral lines.

**Katari** serve as the primary guardians of the Heartwood, a role that traces back to the legendary figure Kael Thornwarden. They are skilled defenders and mediators, and their duties to the sacred forests are treated as a solemn, inherited obligation rather than merely a political arrangement.

**Faeries** inhabit the continent through several distinct Courts — the most prominent being the Courts of Moonlight and Bloom, as well as the mysterious and long-vanished Silent Court. The Faerie Courts maintain complex relationships with mortal peoples: sometimes generous, sometimes dangerous, always operating according to rules and obligations that outsiders rarely understand fully.

**Fauns** are found throughout forested regions and are closely associated with seasonal celebrations, music, and the older pastoral traditions of the continent.

**Galapa** maintain distinct communities throughout the continent, contributing to the patchwork of cultures that makes Ilyndor's social landscape so diverse.

**Fungril** inhabit the deeper, damper portions of the ancient forests, living in communities built around mycelial networks and fungal cultivation.

**Eladrin** are present in portions of Ilyndor, particularly in regions where the boundary between the mortal world and the Faerie realms grows thin.

**Frontier settlers** from [[Eldressa]], [[Velkrand]], and [[Meridian Isles|Meridia]] have established scattered communities in recent centuries, particularly along the northern coast.

Notable wildlife includes creatures associated with the Woodland Guardians and the deep Heartwood — many of which do not exist elsewhere in the world, and many of which are considered sacred by the peoples who share the land with them.

## History

Ilyndor developed very differently from the kingdoms and empires of [[Eldressa]].

The continent's earliest age was presided over by the **Woodland Guardians** — immense primordial beings, neither beast nor god, who embodied aspects of the natural world: forests, rivers, seasons, growth, decay, renewal. Though mortal, they possessed lifespans so vast that entire civilizations could rise and fall within a single Guardian's life. The peoples of Ilyndor often revered them as deities; the Deonati regard them as ancestral progenitors and spiritual forebears.

**Aiyana Spiritwalker** is among the most revered figures in Deonati tradition — said to have been among the first Deonati to recognize the bond between her people and the Woodland Guardians, traveling the continent carrying their teachings between distant tribes.

The **Great Gathering of Antlers** was one of the earliest continent-wide events in Ilyndoran memory, bringing representatives of dozens of Deonati, Katari, Faerie, Faun, and Galapa communities together beneath the boughs of the Heartwood to establish peaceful coexistence. Unlike treaties of other continents, the Gathering was never written down — it was carried forward through song, story, dance, and oral tradition.

The **Path of Falling Leaves** was a historic migration in which several Deonati clans, led by the explorer and spiritual guide **Naru Windtail**, crossed the western seas and established the first lasting Deonati presence in [[Eldressa]]. The descendants of these travelers remain in Eldressa today.

The **Blackroot Blight** remains one of the most significant ecological disasters in Ilyndoran memory — a mysterious corruption that spread through portions of the Heartwood, killing ancient trees and driving several Woodland Guardians to their deaths before it was contained. Small pockets of Blackroot still occasionally appear in remote regions. Many believe the corruption never truly ended. It merely sleeps.

The figure of **Sereth the Thorn King** stands as one of the continent's great cautionary histories — a Katari guardian who became convinced that mortals themselves were the greatest threat to nature, turned to increasingly extreme methods, and had to be stopped by a coalition of other Guardians and Katari before he could destroy settled communities.

## Present Day

Ilyndor in 300 AU has no unified government, and its peoples generally prefer it that way.

The **Woodland Guardians** remain the closest thing the continent possesses to a shared authority. When the Guardians speak together, most peoples of Ilyndor listen. **Aruun Rootfather**, one of the oldest surviving Guardians, serves as the most respected voice among their number.

**Deonati settlements** are the most organized communities on the continent, governed through local councils and elected elders. The **Elder Speaker**, currently **Sayume Riverstep**, serves as a respected diplomat and one of the most influential Deonati leaders alive.

The **Katari of the Heartwood** continue to live primarily in tribal communities centered around their duties as guardians of the sacred forests. **Takoro Leafstride** serves as their Chief Warden and is also a principal representative of the **Jade Current Company** — the dominant trading organization connecting Ilyndor to [[Meridian Isles|Meridia]].

The **Meridian Protectorates** along the northern coast represent a recent and somewhat controversial development: several tropical islands settled by wealthy Meridian interests, operating under a loose administrative coalition. Tensions occasionally arise between these commercial settlements and the older communities of the interior.

Ilyndor exports rare reagents, spirit-touched materials, medicinal plants, exotic woods, and companion animals to the wider world, making it a quiet but significant player in global trade despite its rejection of political centralization.
