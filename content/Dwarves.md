# Dwarves

Dwarves are among the oldest mortal peoples of [[Eldressa]], renowned throughout the known world for engineering, craftsmanship, and an endurance that has seen their civilization survive wars, disasters, and the passage of ages. Their great holds beneath the mountains of Eldressa remain among the most formidable constructed works in mortal history.

---

## Origins

Dwarven civilization took root in the **Claran Mountains** of Eldressa during the earliest ages following the Shattering. The mountains provided natural defensibility, abundant ore and stone, and access to deep places that suited a people who would develop some of the greatest expertise in subterranean construction and mining in the world.

The **Accord of Sky and Stone** — one of the oldest surviving mythic treaties — was reportedly forged between the early Dwarven clans and the Dragon steward **Ithrymath**. The agreement established that the Dwarves would mine beneath the mountains while the Dragon guarded the peaks and deep passages from creatures emerging from the underworld. Many modern Dwarven holds still display broken fragments of tablets said to preserve portions of this ancient accord.

**Thargrim Goldvein — The King Beneath the Mountain** is remembered as one of the earliest great Dwarven rulers. He unified dozens of feuding mountain clans and established the first true subterranean kingdom beneath the Claran Mountains. Legends claim his throne was forged jointly by mortal smiths and Dragons. His tomb has never been found.

---

## Culture & Society

Dwarven culture places immense value on craft, endurance, loyalty to clan, and the preservation of what has been built. Where [[Elves]] measure greatness by age and tradition, Dwarves measure it by what they have constructed and defended.

The Dwarven Holds are governed by councils of clan elders, with a High Chieftain serving as the paramount authority. Decisions made within the council system are deliberate and slow by the standards of other peoples — a reflection of a culture that measures consequences across generations.

Dwarven stonework, metalcraft, and engineering are unmatched among the mortal races. The deep mine networks beneath the Claran Mountains are marvels of construction that have been expanded and maintained across thousands of years. Fortifications built by Dwarven hands are considered among the most difficult siege targets in the world.

The tradition of underground expansion brought early Dwarven civilization into direct conflict with the Elven kingdoms of Eldressa's surface. The **War of Ash and Iron**, fought over control of the **Embervein Range**, neither definitively won nor lost, permanently shaped Dwarf-Elf relations for centuries afterward. The most celebrated moment of that war was the **Battle of Hollow Pass**, where **Highthane Brokkir Stonevein** held a narrow mountain pass against repeated Elven assaults for thirteen days before ultimately collapsing the mountain itself rather than surrendering. Dwarven culture still treats the battle as a symbol of endurance and sacrifice.

---

## Distribution

The Claran and Lanaryan Mountains of [[Eldressa]] remain the heartland of Dwarven civilization, but Dwarves have spread considerably across the known world over the millennia.

**[[Velkrand]]** has significant Dwarven communities, particularly in its mining and industrial regions. Dwarven craftsmanship has been influential in the development of Velkrand's engineering traditions.

Dwarven craftspeople and engineers are found in most major cities of the known world, serving as architects, smiths, fortification specialists, and mining consultants.

---

## Notable Historical Figures

**Thargrim Goldvein — The King Beneath the Mountain** — The first great Dwarven ruler, who unified the mountain clans and established the first true Dwarven kingdom.

**Highthane Brokkir Stonevein** — Commander of the defense at the Battle of Hollow Pass during the War of Ash and Iron. The defining symbol of Dwarven stubbornness and sacrifice.

**Brokk Stoneheart** — Current High Chieftain of the Dwarven Holds and leader of the Seven Elders council.

---

## Present Day

The **Dwarven Holds** of the Claran and Lanaryan Mountains remain among [[Eldressa]]'s most powerful military and industrial forces in 300 AU. **High Chieftain Brokk Stoneheart** leads the council of Seven Elders, maintaining the historically isolationist stance that has characterized the Holds for most of recorded history.

Though the Holds have engaged more actively in Eldressan diplomacy since the Unification — participating in the [[Council of Valtiron]] and expanding trade agreements — they remain cautious about outside entanglement. Their military strength and productive capacity mean their cooperation is sought after by every major power on the continent.

The relationship between the Holds and the rising industrialism of [[Velkrand]] is an ongoing point of interest among political observers. Dwarven engineering expertise and Velkrand's industrial ambition are a natural fit, and cross-continental partnerships between Dwarven craftspeople and Velkrander guilds have increased considerably in the modern era.
