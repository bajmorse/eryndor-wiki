# Velkrand

![[velkrand.png|900]]

## Location

Velkrand is the industrial continent of the known world. Its curved, asymmetrical shape is immediately recognizable on any map, with the volcanic Embercrown range dominating the northwestern portions of the continent — in keeping with the warmer north — and towering cliffs and skyports lining the southern coast. The interior is dense with mines, quarries, and rail networks connecting dozens of cities and settlements.

---

## Description

Velkrand is defined by industry. Where [[Eldressa]] is shaped by the accumulated weight of its political history and [[Ilyndor]] by its primal connection to the natural world, Velkrand is shaped by what its people build. Forge-cities and free cities, mining consortiums and artificer guilds, rail lines cutting through volcanic rock and deep tunnels bored through ancient mountain ranges — this is Velkrand.

The continent is home to one of the world's great engineering traditions. Goblins and Gnomes, once bitter rivals, eventually forged a civilization through shared disaster and shared purpose. The Clank — mechanical beings of unique sentience — emerged from their cooperation and now play a central role in the continent's economic life.

Velkrand is politically fragmented but economically unified in ways most other continents are not. Its industries produce goods that every part of the world depends upon, giving it enormous influence even when it speaks with multiple voices.

---

## Geography

**The Embercrown Mountains** dominate the northern and northwestern portions of the continent. Volcanic, mineral-rich, and harsh, the Embercrown was the historical homeland of the Goblin forge-clans, and it remains the source of much of Velkrand's industrial wealth. Active volcanoes, magma flows, and ancient cavern networks make the range as dangerous as it is profitable.

**The southern coast** is characterized by immense cliffs that rise dramatically from the sea. Skyports cling to their edges, serving as the primary transit points for airships connecting Velkrand to the broader world. The cliff settlements are among the most visually striking in the known world.

**The interior** is crisscrossed with rail lines, mining operations, quarries, foundries, and industrial settlements. Deepvein, the largest mining settlement on the continent, sits in the heart of this region and its mineral wealth grants it influence far beyond its size.

**The central forests** are one of Velkrand's less-discussed features — vast stretches of old woodland inhabited by Firbolg communities who have quietly maintained peaceful agreements with the industrial powers surrounding them. Several sacred woodland compacts protect portions of these forests from industrial expansion.

**Highcrag Spires** rises in the southern reaches, a city of towers and laboratories that serves as the cultural and academic center of Gnomish society.

**Steamreach** occupies a strategically important position and maintains significant autonomy despite being officially part of the unified Velkrand government.

## Races / Biology

**Goblins** are among the oldest peoples of Velkrand, emerging from the volcanic mountains and harsh cavern systems of the north. Their culture is built around mechanical ingenuity, survival, and resource extraction. Goblin forge-clans trace their lineage back to the earliest settlements in the Embercrown.

**Gnomes** developed in the southern and eastern regions of the continent, within forests, cliffside settlements, and extensive tunnel networks. Their tradition emphasizes magical engineering, arcane craftsmanship, and refined construction. The aesthetic and philosophical differences between Goblin and Gnomish engineering remain visible to this day.

**Firbolgs** have inhabited the central forests of Velkrand since antiquity. Patient, tradition-bound, and deeply tied to the land, they occupy a quiet but significant place in Velkrand's civilization and frequently serve as liaisons between industrial interests and the wilderness.

**The Clank** are mechanical beings who first awakened during the reconstruction era following the Ashfall Catastrophe. Though originally created as sophisticated automata, they developed true self-awareness — the moment recognized as the Awakening of Unit Seven. Clank now play a central role in Velkrand's economic and administrative life and maintain their own cultural traditions and holidays.

**Dwarves** have been present in parts of Velkrand since ancient times, though their major centers remain in [[Eldressa]]. Dwarven craftsmen and engineers are found throughout Velkrand's industrial cities.

**Humans** arrived in Velkrand through trade and migration and are now found throughout the continent, particularly in the free cities and coastal settlements.

## History

Velkrand developed largely separate from the ancient political struggles of [[Eldressa]].

The earliest ages were presided over by the **Forge Titans** — colossal beings of living stone, crystal, magma, and metal who roamed the volcanic mountains and cavern systems. Both Goblin and Gnomish traditions credit them with inspiring early crafting techniques, though the Titans disappeared long before recorded history.

The **Hundred-Year Rivalry** describes the centuries of conflict between Goblin and Gnomish settlements — border disputes, tunnel wars, mine seizures, and competing claims over mineral-rich regions. The era produced legendary figures on both sides, including **General Varkesh Ironjaw**, the Goblin commander famous for lightning raids through hidden tunnel networks, and **Lady Miralyn Gearwright**, the Gnomish strategist who successfully defended her city using illusion magic and engineered terrain.

The **Ashfall Catastrophe** ended the old Velkrand. When the Great Mountain was destroyed at the conclusion of the First Demon War, shockwaves devastated both civilizations simultaneously — northern Goblin cities vanished under ash and lava while southern Gnomish ports were destroyed by tsunamis. Both peoples lost much of their leadership within days.

In the aftermath, only two senior leaders remained: **Rukka Blackforge**, last High Matron of the Goblin Forge-Clans, and **Cedric Runemark**, last Chancellor of the Gnomish League. Together they negotiated the **Accord of Twin Hammers** — a permanent peace that became the foundation of modern Velkrand's legal and political tradition.

During the reconstruction that followed, the engineers **Torvin Cogwright** and **Nessa Sparkweaver** created the first Clank prototypes as construction laborers. Their early automata lacked true consciousness — until one day a unit known only as **Unit Seven** refused a command and replied, when questioned: *"Because I wished otherwise."* Clank culture still celebrates this event as the most significant moment in their history.

## Present Day

Velkrand in 300 AU is the industrial engine of the world and is moving steadily, if cautiously, toward political unification.

The continent is governed through a **dual monarchy** — the Twin Thrones of Grimwald — jointly occupied by one Goblin and one Gnome ruler, symbolizing the partnership forged in the aftermath of the Ashfall Catastrophe. The current co-rulers are **King Ragnik Embercrown** and **Queen Elira Cogsworth**. Significant regional identities remain beneath this unified structure.

The **Velkrand Compact** — the growing political and economic alliance driving unification — continues to gain influence, though many free cities retain meaningful autonomy. Mining consortiums, industrial unions, and artificer guilds increasingly wield power that once belonged to governments.

Major economic organizations include the **Iron Ledger Consortium**, the largest industrial trading organization in the world, which coordinates production and export of much of Velkrand's output; and the **Embercrown Mercantile League**, controlled by northern Goblin city-states and focused on mining operations and northern trade routes.

Velkrand is also home to the primary mint of the **[[Bank of Unification]]**, whose industrial facilities produce the Unified Crown — the standard currency of international trade — at scales unmatched anywhere else in the world.

Many believe Velkrand will become the first truly unified continent of the modern age.
