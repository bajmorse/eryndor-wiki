# The Human Migration

The Human Migration refers to the great exodus of [[Humans|human]] tribes from their original homeland in [[Hrothan]] northward into [[Eldressa]] — the most consequential population movement in recorded human history, and one of the defining events of the early Mortal Age.

---

## Background: The Long Winter

For untold generations, the human peoples of [[Hrothan]] built their survival around the **Icehorns** — enormous migratory tundra beasts that provided food, leather, bone, shelter materials, and fuel. The herds were vast enough that entire tribal cultures oriented around following their seasonal migrations across the Pale Expanse.

Then, without sufficient warning to prepare, the herds began to collapse.

Different traditions offer different explanations: disease, altered migration routes caused by the Colossi, an ancient curse, a shift in the climate. Whatever the cause, the collapse of the Icehorn herds — known as the **Long Winter** — brought famine across [[Hrothan]].

Human tribes that had previously coexisted with Goliath clans and Giant territories through a rough equilibrium now competed desperately over vanishing resources. The **Battle of Broken Tusks** — one of the largest conflicts of the Long Winter, fought when starving human tribes tried to follow the last major Icehorn migration into Goliath territory — convinced many leaders that Hrothan could no longer indefinitely support all its peoples.

**Lyra Snowsong — The Last Voice of the North** spent the worst years of the Long Winter traveling between scattered tribes, gathering songs, genealogies, and stories she feared would be lost entirely. Many of the human oral histories that survive today descend from the Snowsongs she composed.

---

## The Decision to Leave

The **Gathering of a Thousand Fires** — a remarkable moment of peaceful assembly on the shores of [[The Great Frozen Lake|Lake Varglas]] — brought together representatives of human tribes, Goliath clans, and Giant lineages to settle old feuds, arrange marriages, establish trade routes, and exchange what knowledge remained. For seven nights, thousands of fires burned across the frozen lake.

The Gathering did not stop the Long Winter. But it helped establish the principle that leaving was not abandonment — it was a choice made by those who could no longer feed their children in the land that had always been home.

The **Last Hunt** — when representatives of Humans, Goliaths, and Giants tracked the final great Icehorn herd together and feasted communally rather than fighting over the remaining animals — is treated across many traditions as the symbolic close of the old Hrothan. After the Last Hunt, the direction was set.

---

## Kaelor Icewalker and the First Crossing

The central figure of the Migration is **Kaelor Icewalker**, the legendary tribal leader credited with leading the first great human migration northward into [[Eldressa]].

Kaelor is remembered not primarily as a warrior but as a guide and pathfinder. He is said to have spent years before the main migration mapping routes through glaciers, frozen coastlines, and mountain passes — identifying a safe corridor across the **Frostfire Strait**, the narrow frozen channel separating Hrothan from the southern lands.

Most depictions show him carrying a lantern said to contain dragonfire gifted by an ancient wyrm — a detail that appears across dozens of independent cultural traditions, suggesting either a genuine encounter with a Dragon or a story so compelling it was universally adopted. Some historians believe a Dragon guide was genuinely involved; the Age of Dragon Stewardship was still active, and Dragons were known to assist mortal peoples.

Whether Kaelor was a single historical individual or a composite of many leaders across many crossings is debated. What is not debated is the crossing itself. Thousands of humans crossed the Frostfire Strait. Many perished. Those who survived eventually became the ancestors of most human peoples living in [[Eldressa]] today.

Among human cultures, the Crossing is often described as the moment humanity chose hope over survival.

---

## Arrival in Eldressa

The human peoples who arrived in [[Eldressa]] entered a world already shaped by thousands of years of Elven and Dwarven civilization. The integration of these newcomers into Eldressan society was neither simple nor bloodless, but it was ultimately transformative.

Over the following centuries, human settlements spread across Eldressa's plains, coasts, and river valleys. Humans proved adaptable in ways that accelerated their expansion — willing to integrate into existing trading networks, adopt technologies from neighbors, and intermarry across cultural lines where other peoples maintained stricter boundaries.

The arrival of humans permanently altered the demographic and political character of [[Eldressa]], eventually making them the continent's largest single population and a primary force in its complex political landscape.

---

## Legacy

The Human Migration left a physical record across both continents. In [[Hrothan]], ancient migration routes are still marked by cairns, standing stones, burial mounds, and weathered campsites — the accumulated physical memory of generations of movement. In [[Eldressa]], the foundations of numerous settlements and several of the oldest roads trace back to the earliest human arrivals.

The story of the Migration remains central to human cultural identity across the world. It is a story about what people do when the world they know can no longer support them — and about what is lost and what is found in the crossing.
