# Nyx Ashwisk

![[nyx-ashwisk.png|300]]

**Race:** Katari
**Class:** Hedge Witch
**Origin:** Nomadic — regularly passes through Sky-Bough Village

Nyx Ashwisk is a nomadic Katari Hedge Witch and apothecary who regularly passes through Sky-Bough Village. She keeps a small white ferret familiar, [[Snowberry]], bound to her.
