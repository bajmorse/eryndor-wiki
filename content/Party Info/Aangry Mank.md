# Aangry Mank

![[aangry-mank.png|300]]

**Race:** Simiah
**Class:** Beastbound Ranger
**Origin:** Sky-Bough Village

Aangry Mank is a Simiah Beastbound Ranger and a native of Sky-Bough Village. He travels with his loyal companion, the albino buffalo [[Buffa-lo Bill]], at his side.
