# Session 0

## Setting

Sky-Bough Village is a predominantly Kenku settlement built high among the colossal trees of an ancient forest in southwestern Ilyndor, near the Summering Sea between the Windrider Cliffs and Frostfang Mountains. Simiah make up much of the remaining population, alongside smaller numbers of Katari and other peoples.

At the heart of village life is the Bent Feather Tavern, run by Joe the Barkeep, brewmaster of the famous Skybound Pale Ale. Joe brews it using water from the nearby Sacred Spring, while Sky-Bough's young Kenku mayor controls its trade and distribution.

## The Party

Gathered at their usual table in the Bent Feather Tavern, the party are already well-known to each other as regular drinking companions rather than adventurers brought together by fate:

- [[Aangry Mank]] — Sky-Bough native, accompanied by his albino buffalo, [[Buffa-lo Bill]].
- [[Finnegan Endercast]] — longtime resident of Sky-Bough.
- [[Frayne Castel]] — from nearby Goldhorn Crossing, whose family was killed when that village was destroyed in a territorial conflict.
- [[Nyx Ashwisk]] — a nomadic apothecary who regularly passes through Sky-Bough.
- [[Zella Ironstone]] — born in the Frostfang Mountains, frequently visiting during her travels.

They're not adventurers brought together by destiny — they're five people who already know each other, drink together whenever they're all in town, and are now watching a place they care about slowly come apart.

## What Happened

The party met at the Bent Feather Tavern over pints of Skybound Pale Ale, catching each other up on the gossip, news, and goings-on they'd each encountered since they last met.

## Town Troubles & Rumors

What the party learned or overheard during the session:

- **The harvest is failing.** Farms surrounding Sky-Bough are producing less food, creating shortages throughout the village. Even the beloved Parker family, famous for endlessly giving away pastries, is struggling to obtain ingredients.
- **The Sacred Spring is drying up.** Its waters supposedly slow aging and are the secret ingredient in Skybound Pale Ale. Falling water levels mean Joe can produce increasingly little ale.
- **Skybound Pale Ale is disappearing.** The mayor has been giving visiting merchants and foreign contacts preferential access to the dwindling supply. Resentment toward outsiders is growing, while many villagers blame Joe for the shortage and believe he is trying to maintain his influence over the village.
- **Interest in the martial arts tournament is fading.** Joe's monthly tournament — whose winner represents Sky-Bough as dignitary for the following month — is drawing fewer spectators and fighters than before. Some mutter it's because Joe uses it to control who gets to represent the village.
- **The Ancient Pact seems to have failed.** For generations, Sky-Bough has sacrificed one randomly selected villager each year in exchange for protection and prosperous harvests. Last year's sacrifice escaped and remains missing. Many now believe the failed harvest, drying spring, and increasingly dangerous wilderness are consequences.
- **Children are disappearing.** Every known victim has been a minor, and a strange symbol is found near each disappearance. Rumors blame the eccentric relic collector Old Man Willy, whom villagers already suspect of being a necromancer.
- **The Summering Sea is becoming dangerous.** Coastal fishing settlements have been attacked by supposed "Sea People." Whether these are organized attackers, raiders, refugees, or people fleeing something worse remains unclear. Some believe they are responsible for disrupting the pact — or that the escaped sacrifice fled to them.
- **An ancient sealed tomb has been rediscovered.** Long ago, a traveling sorcerer supposedly saved Sky-Bough by sealing away a demon and promising to return someday to destroy it. The sorcerer never returned. Land clearance has now uncovered the tomb, and local priests intend to open it "for research purposes."
- **The Testers are growing.** The strange religion dedicated to Sir Testalot recently interpreted a moustache-shaped sign in the sky as pointing toward the Summering Sea. Their beliefs may have unexpected connections to an older cult whose followers committed mass suicide in the Sacred Spring generations ago.

## Open Questions

- Who or what escaped the Ancient Pact last year, and where did they go?
- Who is behind the disappearance of the village's children?
- Are the "Sea People" connected to the pact, the tomb, or something else entirely?
- What will the priests find when they open the sealed tomb?
- Is Joe really using the tournament to control who represents Sky-Bough — and if so, why?
