# Eldressa

![[eldressa.png|900]]

## Location

Eldressa is the historical and cultural heart of civilization, and the most populous and politically influential continent in the known world. It lies at the center of recorded history, and most of the oldest surviving myths, kingdoms, and institutions trace their origins to its soil. It is positioned within comfortable sailing distance of the [[Meridian Isles]] and maintains active trade connections with [[Velkrand]].

---

## Description

Eldressa is a fertile, diverse, and deeply layered continent shaped by thousands of years of civilization, conflict, and cooperation. Unlike the industrial engines of [[Velkrand]] or the primal wilds of [[Ilyndor]], Eldressa is defined by the accumulated weight of its history — ancient forests, ruined kingdoms, well-worn trade roads, and cities that have changed hands more times than anyone can reliably count.

It is not a unified nation. Power is fragmented among dozens of sovereign states, kingdoms, city-states, and regional governments, many of which trace their origins back to the earliest ages of mortal civilization. The continent functions as much through diplomacy, trade, and cultural influence as through any formal authority.

More than anywhere else in the world, Eldressa is where the oldest stories happened.

## Geography

Eldressa encompasses several distinct geographic regions, each with its own character.

**The Woods of Brethil** are among the most ancient living forests in the world. Long sacred to the Elves, the deep groves of Brethil are protected by a centuries-old oath forbidding warfare within them. Ancient white stone memorials still mark the graves of those who fell when that oath was once broken.

**The Claran Mountains** rise across a significant portion of the continent and have been home to Dwarven holds since the earliest ages of mortal civilization. The mountains are rich in ore, crystal deposits, and ancient ruins, and they remain the heartland of Dwarven culture.

**The Lanaryan Desert** stretches across warmer inland regions, a vast and largely inhospitable expanse that has nonetheless served as the backdrop for ancient trade routes and forgotten empires.

**The Danac Hills and Florian Plains** form the agricultural backbone of central Eldressa. The rolling hills and open farmland support dense populations and a thriving trade culture historically associated with the Halflings.

**The Embervein Range** is a contested mountain chain historically fought over by both Elven and Dwarven powers. Several ruined fortresses from ancient wars still stand abandoned among its peaks.

**Rivara** sits on the coast, one of the great port cities of the world, commanding enormous commercial influence through its harbor and merchant houses.

**Coral** is a coastal city of independent character, prosperous and cosmopolitan, resisting formal incorporation into any single political structure.

**Valtiron** was constructed within the ancient crater left by the destruction of the Great Mountain during the First Demon War. It now serves as the political meeting place of the continent.

## Races / Biology

Eldressa is the most racially diverse continent in the world, a consequence of its long history as the center of civilization and trade.

**Elves** represent some of the oldest continuous cultures on the continent. Their kingdoms arose within Brethil and the river valleys long before most other mortal civilizations took shape. Several distinct Elven peoples exist, including High Elves, Wood Elves, Sea Elves, and Drow, each with their own traditions and political identities.

**Dwarves** have inhabited the Claran Mountains since antiquity, building their great holds beneath the stone and developing reputations for engineering, craftsmanship, and military endurance that remain unmatched.

**Halflings** spread across the Danac Hills and Florian Plains, establishing trade routes and caravan cultures that helped knit the continent together in its earliest ages. Many of the oldest roads in Eldressa follow paths that Halfling traders first marked.

**Humans** arrived in Eldressa from [[Hrothan]] during the First Age, following the great migration led by Kaelor Icewalker across the frozen strait. They are now among the most widespread peoples on the continent.

**Deonati** have maintained communities in Eldressa since the Path of Falling Leaves brought their ancestors westward from [[Ilyndor]] long before recorded history. They were also subject to centuries of persecution and displacement, the full effects of which their societies are still recovering from.

**Demons** settled across Eldressa following the peace accords at the end of the Second Demon War. Their integration into Eldressan society has been gradual but significant, and several Demon individuals have risen to prominent roles in politics, commerce, and culture.

## History

Eldressa was the first great cradle of mortal civilization following the Shattering.

In the earliest ages, the **Dragons of Eldressa** served as direct stewards of the emerging mortal races, guiding the development of kingdoms, mediating disputes between peoples, and teaching craftsmanship and agriculture. This era is remembered as the Age of Dragon Stewardship — one of the most celebrated periods in Eldressan myth.

The **War of Ash and Iron** was among the first great mortal conflicts, fought between early Elven kingdoms and expanding Dwarven mountain holds over control of the Embervein Range. Neither side achieved decisive victory, but the war permanently shaped Elven-Dwarven relations for centuries.

The **Crownless War** was an Elven civil conflict fought over succession following the death of Queen Althiriel Vaelor. It became deeply philosophical — arguments over the role of magic, the preservation of ancient tradition, and relations with younger races — and its divisions fractured Elven unity in ways still felt today. The **Weeping of Brethil** stands as its darkest chapter, when rival houses violated the sacred forests, and the surviving leaders swore the Oath of Falling Leaves in response.

The **First Demon War** reshaped the continent catastrophically. The destruction of the Great Mountain sent shockwaves across the world. Its crater was eventually chosen as the site of Valtiron, transforming ruin into political purpose.

The **Second Demon War** brought further devastation, particularly to the southern regions of the continent. It ended with the Unification — the sealing of the Void Rift and the establishment of peace between mortals and Demons — marking the beginning of the Modern Age.

## Present Day

Eldressa in 300 AU is not an empire, nor any single nation. It is a collection of sovereign powers, old kingdoms, and independent cities that have learned — sometimes painfully — to coexist.

**[[Valtiron]]** serves as the political heart of the continent. Built within the crater of the Great Mountain, it operates as neutral ground where representatives from across Eldressa and beyond gather to negotiate treaties, resolve disputes, and coordinate on matters affecting the wider world. The current Chancellor, **Serazeth Vael**, is the first Demon to hold that office and is widely regarded as one of the most skilled diplomats alive.

**[[Rivara]]** remains one of the world's most influential financial centers. Though technically governed by a Magistrate, its merchant houses and trade guilds wield enormous power. Its wealth derives from banking, maritime trade, and commercial networks that span every continent.

**The Kingdom of Brethil**, ruled by **Queen Aeloria Starbloom**, one of the oldest political leaders in the world, holds enormous cultural and magical influence. The sacred forests remain protected by ancient oaths and the weight of Elven tradition.

**The Dwarven Holds** of the Claran and Lanaryan Mountains, led by **High Chieftain Brokk Stoneheart**, remain among Eldressa's most powerful military and industrial forces, though they retain their historically isolationist tendencies.

**The Kingdom of Algade** represents the dominant Human political power on the continent, overseeing several subsidiary settlements and serving as the primary voice of Human interests within Eldressan diplomacy.

The continent is also home to the **[[Council of Valtiron]]**, the closest institution the world possesses to an international governing body — influential but possessing limited direct authority, functioning as a mixture of diplomatic forum and cooperative assembly.
