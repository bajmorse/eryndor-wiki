# Meridia

Meridia is the largest city in the known world and its diplomatic and economic capital. Located on the largest island of the [[Meridian Isles]], it sits at the intersection of every major maritime trade route and serves as the neutral ground where the great powers of Eryndor meet, negotiate, and conduct the business of the world.

No single power controls Meridia. Its neutrality is considered one of its greatest assets — and is treated by the major powers as something worth protecting, because every one of them needs it to function.

---

## Character

Meridia is not a city of ancient tradition. It grew from a modest port into one of the world's largest cities within the space of roughly two centuries during the Age of Discovery, when expanding trade routes made the [[Meridian Isles|Freewaters]] the natural crossroads of global commerce.

The speed of that growth is visible in its character. Meridia is cosmopolitan, transactional, and relentlessly diverse. Its neighborhoods are defined by trade and origin rather than ancient custom — a Velkrander merchant quarter sits near a Deonati cultural district; an Eldressan banking house operates across the street from a Hrothan fur warehouse. Every major people of Eryndor is present. Every major language is spoken.

The city is also, by reputation, ruthlessly practical. Questions of ancient lineage, old loyalties, or cultural tradition carry less weight here than competence, connection, and the ability to close a deal. This makes Meridia uncomfortable for those accustomed to societies where birth determines status, and enormously attractive to those who prefer to be judged on results.

## Governance

Meridia is officially governed by a **Governess**, though the city's actual political life is considerably more complex. The Merchant Council, composed of the major commercial interests operating in the city, holds substantial influence over daily affairs and economic policy.

**Governess Admiral Celeste Fairwind (Human, Female)** is a former sailor, explorer, and merchant captain who rose to lead the city through a combination of pragmatic competence and genuine diplomatic skill. She is widely respected for her commitment to maintaining Meridia's neutrality — a commitment she treats as a fundamental political principle rather than merely a policy.

**Merchant Council Speaker Mira Goodbarrel (Halfling, Female)** is one of the wealthiest individuals in the world and the most influential voice in Meridia's commercial administration. Her priorities are rarely identical to the Governess's, but the two maintain a functional working relationship that most observers regard as essential to the city's stability.

## Key Institutions

**[[Bank of Unification]]** — The Bank's headquarters relocated to Meridia from [[Rivara]] during the Age of Discovery, following the commercial weight of the city as it became the center of global trade. The Bank administers the Unified Crown and maintains branches across the known world. Its Meridian director, **Selene Fairmarket**, coordinates international banking operations.

**Dragon Rider Embassies** — [[Dragons]] maintain diplomatic presence in Meridia, making it one of the few places where a traveler can reliably reach Dragon Rider communications without prior arrangement. The embassies serve as neutral ground for messages and diplomatic contacts that cross continental lines.

**International Guilds** — Dozens of major trade guilds, professional organizations, and international bodies maintain offices in Meridia. The concentration of institutional presence here is unmatched anywhere else in the world.

**The Jade Current Company** — The dominant trading organization connecting Meridia with [[Ilyndor]], specializing in luxury goods, rare reagents, passenger transport, and cultural exchange. Its Meridian Director of Operations is **Evelyn Fairwind**.

**The Northern Star Trading Company** — The principal organization maintaining trade with [[Hrothan]] and [[Skeldran]], operating some of the hardiest fleets in the world through the dangerous Dragon Waters.

## Relationship with Nadris

Meridia's shadow is **Nadris** — the rival port city on a neighboring island, governed by three Pirate Lords and dedicated to the principle that commerce should be free of institutional oversight.

The two cities are officially rivals. Practically, they are economically inseparable. Goods that cannot move through Meridia's regulated channels flow through Nadris. Interests that prefer anonymity use Nadris's networks. The black markets and smuggling operations of Nadris service needs that Meridia's formal economy cannot or will not acknowledge.

Most experienced merchants and officials in Meridia are quietly grateful that Nadris exists. The alternative — trying to absorb that commerce into Meridia's regulatory framework — would be far more disruptive than the current arrangement.

---

## Present Day

In 300 AU, Meridia is at the height of its influence. No single event in the modern era has challenged its position as the world's diplomatic and commercial hub.

The city continues to grow. New neighborhoods expand onto reclaimed land at the harbor's edges. New peoples arrive with new goods and new traditions. New problems — the expanding reach of the [[Velkrand Compact]], the commercial pressure on [[Hrothan]]'s tribal lands, the ongoing questions around Demon integration — arrive at Meridia's diplomatic tables to be argued over, because Meridia is where the world's arguments happen.
