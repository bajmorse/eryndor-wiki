# The Dragon Sacrifice

> [!info] Canon Status
> The Dragon Sacrifice is a significant historical event whose details are currently under development. What follows represents the fragmentary record as it is currently understood.

The Dragon Sacrifice refers to a pivotal and poorly understood event — or series of events — associated with the [[Dragons|Dragons']] long withdrawal from the mortal world. It is believed to be connected to the end of the [[The First Demon War|Demon Wars]] and the centuries-long absence of Dragonkind from Eryndor that preceded the Age of Reconnection.

---

## What Is Known

Following the Age of Dragon Stewardship, the [[Dragons]] who had served as guides and guardians of mortal civilization began to withdraw. The reasons for this withdrawal are among the most debated questions in historical scholarship.

What the historical record preserves is:

- [[Dragons]] were actively present during the early mortal age, guiding civilizations across [[Eldressa]] and beyond.
- At some point during or after the conflicts of the Demon War era, [[Dragons]] largely disappeared from the world.
- Their absence lasted for centuries — long enough that the returning Dragons of the Age of Reconnection were encountered as something genuinely new by most peoples alive at the time.
- When asked about the period of their absence, Dragons in the modern era are notably reluctant to discuss the specifics. Most defer to the shame their culture still carries from the Silence of Dragons during the Celestial War, but whether the Dragon Sacrifice and that ancient failure are directly connected remains unclear.

---

## Scholarly Theories

Several interpretations of the Dragon Sacrifice circulate among historians and scholars.

**The Atonement Theory** holds that the Dragon Sacrifice was a deliberate act of collective self-diminishment — that [[Dragons]], weighted by their failure during the Celestial War and their complicated role in the Demon Wars, chose to sacrifice some portion of their power, presence, or numbers as a form of cosmic penance. The return of the Dragons in the Age of Reconnection, on this interpretation, represents the completion of that atonement.

**The Necessity Theory** holds that [[Dragons]] sacrificed themselves or their presence in the world for a specific practical purpose — sealing the Void Rift, ending the Second Demon War, or stabilizing some cosmological damage that the mortal races were not equipped to address. This interpretation is popular among those who view the Unification's sealing of the Void Rift and the Dragons' return as two aspects of the same completed process.

**The Withdrawal Theory** holds that "sacrifice" is a mortal framing of what was simply a prolonged Dragon retreat — that [[Dragons]], for their own reasons, chose to remove themselves from mortal affairs for several centuries, and that their return was equally voluntary.

None of these theories is definitively established. The [[Dragons]] themselves have not provided authoritative public accounts of this period.

---

## Connection to the Modern Era

What is notable is that the Age of Reconnection — when Dragons returned to the world — coincided directly with the aftermath of the Unification and the beginning of the Modern Age. Whether this timing was causal or coincidental is among the great open questions of contemporary scholarship.

The Dragon Riders, who emerged during the Age of Reconnection as the world's primary long-distance communication network, have become so integrated into modern life that it is easy to forget their existence spans less than two centuries. The world before them — the world in which Dragons were absent — was a fundamentally different and more isolated place.

Understanding the Dragon Sacrifice fully may be essential to understanding what the return of the Dragons truly means.
