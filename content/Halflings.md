# Halflings

Halflings are a people defined by movement, trade, and hospitality. Historically centered in the **Danac Hills** and **Florian Plains** of [[Eldressa]], they established the trade networks and caravan routes that helped knit the continent together in its earliest ages. Today they are found across the known world, occupying roles in commerce, diplomacy, agriculture, and exploration wherever there are goods to exchange and roads to walk.

---

## Origins

Halfling origins in the mortal age are tied closely to the fertile lowlands of central [[Eldressa]]. The rolling hills and open plains of the Danac Hills and Florian Plains provided ideal conditions for agriculture and, critically, for the development of overland trade — the activity that would come to define Halfling culture for millennia.

The legendary figure of **Elyra Windfoot — The Lantern Queen** is credited with establishing many of the earliest formal trade routes across central Eldressa. A Halfling explorer, diplomat, and caravan leader, she is said to have traveled farther than any mortal of her age and carried a lantern that never extinguished. She is regarded as the spiritual founder of the wandering caravan culture still common among Halflings today. Roadside inns, waystones, and travelers' shrines throughout Eldressa still bear her symbol.

---

## Culture & Society

Halfling culture prizes movement, exchange, and the cultivation of good relationships. A Halfling caravan arriving at a settlement brings not just trade goods but news, songs, stories, and the opportunity for connection with the wider world. This social function made Halflings welcome in most communities and gave them access and influence that transcended their population size.

Halfling communities are typically organized around extended family networks and trade guilds rather than hereditary nobility or military power. Authority comes from reputation, accumulated relationships, and demonstrated competence in commerce and navigation. A respected elder Halfling trader may command more effective influence than a minor king, because their word carries weight across a dozen cities.

The tradition of hospitality runs deep. Halfling settlements and inns are renowned for welcoming travelers regardless of origin, and many of the most famous waystations and travelers' hostels throughout [[Eldressa]] and the [[Meridian Isles]] are Halfling-operated.

Agriculture is the other pillar of Halfling identity. The Danac Hills and Florian Plains produce some of the finest agricultural output in Eldressa, and Halfling farming traditions include techniques developed and refined over thousands of years.

---

## Distribution

Halflings have spread far beyond their original homelands in Eldressa's lowlands.

In the **[[Meridian Isles]]**, Halflings are heavily represented in Meridia's merchant and financial communities. Their social intelligence and commercial acumen make them natural brokers in the world's most diverse trading city.

**Mira Goodbarrel**, the Merchant Council Speaker of [[Meridia]], is one of the wealthiest individuals in the world and a prominent example of the influence Halflings have accumulated in the global economy.

In **[[Eldressa]]**, Halflings remain present throughout the Danac Hills and Florian Plains and are well-represented in [[Rivara]]'s commercial networks.

---

## Notable Historical Figures

**Elyra Windfoot — The Lantern Queen** — Explorer, diplomat, and caravan leader credited with establishing the earliest formal trade routes across Eldressa. Regarded as the spiritual founder of Halfling caravan culture.

**Mira Goodbarrel** — Current Merchant Council Speaker of Meridia and one of the wealthiest individuals alive. Also serves as Director of Investments for the Argent Exchange.

**Hilda Underbough** — Treasurer of the [[Bank of Unification]], widely regarded as one of the most financially astute individuals alive.

**Tovan Reedwalker** — Speaker of Yotl, representing the independent Halfling community within Eldressa's political landscape.

---

## Present Day

Halflings in 300 AU occupy influential roles across the global economy disproportionate to their population. Their historical role as the connective tissue of Eldressan trade has translated naturally into the modern era of international commerce.

In [[Eldressa]], **Yotl** remains a politically distinct and independent Halfling community led by **Tovan Reedwalker**, maintaining the tradition of local self-determination that has characterized Halfling governance throughout history.

In [[Meridia]], Halfling merchants, bankers, and brokers are among the most powerful commercial figures in the world's most important trading city.

The Halfling tradition of maintaining good relationships across political, racial, and cultural lines has made them valuable diplomatic intermediaries in an era where the major powers of [[Eldressa]], [[Velkrand]], and the Meridian Isles are navigating the complex interdependencies of global trade.
