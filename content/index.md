# Welcome to Eryndor

*A world of ancient races, forgotten wars, and untamed frontiers.*

The lands of Eryndor are vast and diverse. From the fractured kingdoms of [[Eldressa]], to the forge-cities of [[Velkrand]], the primal wilderness of [[Ilyndor]], the storm-shattered cliffs of [[Skeldran]], the frozen wastes of [[Hrothan]], and the bustling ports of the [[Meridian Isles]], countless stories await discovery.

## Current Age

The modern era is one of exploration, diplomacy, and uncertainty. Ancient powers stir once more, trade routes connect distant continents, and new alliances form while old rivalries endure.

No empire rules Eryndor.

Instead, the world is shaped by merchants, adventurers, diplomats, dragons, tribal elders, industrial guilds, and forces far older than civilization itself.
