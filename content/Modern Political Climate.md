# Modern Political Climate

The modern world of Eryndor is defined by a delicate and historically unprecedented balance of political, economic, and cultural influence. No single nation, continent, or people dominates. Power is distributed among regional governments, merchant coalitions, tribal confederations, diplomatic institutions, and influential individuals — connected by trade routes, Dragon Rider communications, and the shared interest of an increasingly interdependent world.

The roughly three centuries since the **Unification** have been the most stable in recorded mortal history. No conflict approaching the scale of the Demon Wars has occurred. That stability, however, is not permanence. Ancient rivalries persist beneath the surface of diplomacy, and forces far older than any current institution remain active in the world.

---

## Three Centers of Power

Three regions dominate modern world affairs, each through a different form of influence.

### [[Eldressa]] — Political and Cultural Influence

[[Eldressa]] is the most politically complex continent in the world. Its fragmented structure — dozens of sovereign states, kingdoms, and city-states bound together through diplomacy and shared history rather than any central authority — gives it influence through institutional weight rather than unified power.

**[[Valtiron]]** serves as the political heart of Eldressa and, increasingly, of the wider world. Decisions made within its halls shape the future of the continent. Chancellor **Serazeth Vael** — the first Demon to hold the position — is widely regarded as one of the most skilled diplomats alive.

**[[Rivara]]** is one of the world's most influential financial centers, whose merchant houses and banking networks extend across every continent.

**[[Eldressa]]'s** long cultural history also gives it soft power that cannot be easily quantified — the oldest universities, the most established arcane traditions, and the most deeply embedded diplomatic customs all originate here.

### [[Velkrand]] — Industrial and Technological Influence

[[Velkrand]]'s power is material. It produces the machinery, refined metals, rail technology, and artificer goods that the rest of the world depends on. The continent's industrial output is unmatched, and its influence over the global economy grows with every decade.

The **[[Velkrand Compact]]** — the growing political alliance pushing toward continental unification — represents a potential consolidation of this industrial power into a unified political force. If Velkrand achieves full unification, it would represent the first truly unified continent in the modern age, with the combined economic and industrial weight that implies.

### [[Meridia]] — Diplomatic and Economic Influence

[[Meridia]] is where the world's business is conducted. The largest city in the known world, it serves as a neutral meeting ground for every major power and hosts the headquarters of the **[[Bank of Unification]]** — the closest institution Eryndor has to a central bank.

Governess **Admiral Celeste Fairwind**'s commitment to maintaining Meridia's neutrality is one of the city's most important political assets. Every major power needs Meridia to function, which gives the city leverage that no military or political institution could match.

## Other Major Powers

### [[Ilyndor]]
[[Ilyndor]] has no centralized government and no unified political voice. Its peoples — the [[Deonati]], the Katari, the Faerie Courts, and others — interact with the wider world primarily through trade and the authority of the **Woodland Guardians**. The continent's exports of rare reagents, spirit-touched materials, and medicinal plants give it economic significance despite its political decentralization.

### [[Hrothan]]
[[Hrothan]] remains the least centralized continent in the world. Its Human tribes, Goliath clans, and Giant territories have no shared government and limited interest in international politics. Commercial interests from [[Meridia]] have begun moving into the continent — a development creating tension with the older populations.

### [[Skeldran]]
[[Skeldran]] is effectively ungoverned and is widely considered uninhabited outside of the small settlement of Greywatch. It participates in the global economy only through the salvaged relics and raw materials extracted by expeditions.

## Major Institutions

**[[Council of Valtiron]]** — The primary international diplomatic body. Convenes in Valtiron. Possesses significant moral and political influence, but limited direct enforcement authority. Functions as a mixture of diplomatic forum and cooperative assembly.

**[[Bank of Unification]]** — The world's primary financial institution, headquartered in Meridia. Administers the Unified Crown, the standard currency of international trade.

**Dragon Riders** — The primary communication and diplomatic network connecting the continents. Established following the return of the [[Dragons]] during the Age of Reconnection.

## Key Tensions

Despite the stability of the Modern Age, several ongoing tensions shape political life.

**Demon integration** — Three centuries after the Unification, [[Demons]] are established members of Eldressan and Meridian society. Prejudice and mistrust persist in some regions and communities. The appointment of Chancellor Serazeth Vael is both a symbol of how far integration has come and a reminder of how recently it would have been unthinkable.

**Velkrand unification** — The [[Velkrand Compact]]'s drive toward continental unification is watched closely by other powers. A unified Velkrand would represent a significant shift in the global balance of industrial and political power.

**Commercial expansion into Hrothan** — The arrival of Meridian-backed commercial interests in [[Hrothan]] — most visibly the Cliffwarden Timber Company — raises questions about what outside powers owe to the peoples of the lands they operate in.

**The returning Dragons** — The [[Dragons]] have been back for approximately two centuries. Their role in the modern world is still being defined, and not everyone is comfortable with the influence the Dragon Rider network has accumulated over global communications.
