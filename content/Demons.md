# Demons

Demons are the descendants of the Angels of Gontkhael — the great immortal beings of the Celestial Age who fought the Devils across the dying worlds of the ancient cosmos. The Angels won the Celestial War. In winning it, they lost everything: their world, their immortality, their forms, and the ancient cycle of existence that had sustained reality for untold ages. The beings who emerged from the Shattering were not Angels. They were something new, scarred by catastrophe and estranged from the people they had once been.

---

## Origins

The Angels of Gontkhael were among the three great immortal powers of the Celestial Age, associated with creation, preservation, flourishing life, and continuity. When the Celestial War between Angels and Devils threatened to consume both worlds, the Angels made the desperate decision to harness the **Soulstone** — the metaphysical heart of their world Gontkhael — as a final weapon.

The Soulstone shattered.

Gontkhael broke apart.

The surviving Angels were permanently transformed. Their once radiant forms became scarred by lingering Soulstone energy. Their immortality faded. Their wings darkened. Their souls were permanently fractured.

The event became known as the **Shattering**, and the immediate aftermath — when the surviving Angels awoke to find their world destroyed and their own forms forever changed — is remembered in Demon tradition as **The Ashen Dawn**.

These transformed Angels became the beings now called Demons.

---

## Identity and Memory

Modern Demons preserve fragments of ancient Angelic culture, language, and philosophy, though much has been lost over the millennia since the Shattering. The distance between their current existence and the Angelic world they descend from is a source of ongoing cultural and spiritual complexity.

Many Demons view themselves not as victorious survivors of the Celestial War, but as descendants of a people who won at an incomprehensible cost. Among many Demon traditions, the war is remembered not as a triumph but as the death of two worlds and the collapse of the balance that once sustained existence itself.

**The Broken Heaven** — the drifting fragments of shattered Gontkhael, still technically accessible but dead and ruined — holds deep spiritual significance in many Demon traditions. **Nythorak**, the world of the Devils that was banished into the Void, is also mourned in many Demon cultures, because the Demons understand perhaps better than anyone what it means to lose a world.

Ancient Demon myths and mourning rituals tied to the destruction of the Celestial Age are still practiced in some communities, maintained across thousands of years through a cultural commitment to not forgetting.

---

## History in the Mortal World

Following the Shattering, Demons eventually found their way to Eryndor. The circumstances of their arrival and their relationship with the mortal peoples of Eryndor during the long ages of the First Mortal Era are connected to the **First Demon War** and the **Second Demon War** — both periods under ongoing historical revision.

What is established is that the Second Demon War ended with the **Unification**: a peace accord between the peoples of Eryndor and the Demons, accompanied by the sealing of the Void Rift. This event marks Year 0 of the modern calendar.

In the aftermath of the Unification, many Demons chose to remain in Eryndor rather than return to the dying remnants of shattered Gontkhael. Though tensions between Demon communities and mortal peoples remained significant in the Reconstruction Era, shared hardship and the passage of time led to increasing cooperation and integration.

---

## Culture & Society

Demon culture varies considerably between communities and regions, shaped both by the fragments of ancient Angelic tradition that survived and by the mortal civilizations with which Demons have lived for the past three centuries.

Demon physical appearance varies — scarred, transformed forms, darkened wings, features that carry visible echoes of the Shattering's damage — but the degree and character of transformation differs considerably between individuals and lineages.

Many Demon communities maintain deep scholarly traditions, preserving what fragments of Angelic history, language, and philosophy have survived the millennia. Others have integrated more fully into mortal cultures, their Demon heritage present but not the defining center of their identity.

The spiritual traditions surrounding the Ashen Dawn, the Broken Heaven, and the lost cycle of souls are among the most complex religious philosophies in the known world.

---

## Present Day

In 300 AU, Demons are present throughout [[Eldressa]] and in significant numbers in [[Meridia]] and other major cities of the known world. Three centuries after the Unification, they have become integrated participants in the societies and economies of the modern age, though prejudice and tension persist in some regions.

**Chancellor Serazeth Vael** of [[Valtiron]] is the most prominent Demon political figure in the world — a direct descendant of a figure from the peace accords and the first Demon to serve as Chancellor of Valtiron. She is widely regarded as one of the most skilled diplomats alive.

**Zarinesha**, Director of International Trade for the Argent Exchange, is one of the most visible Demon business leaders in the modern world and a major advocate for continued cooperation between Demons and mortals.

**Zarveth**, Auditor at the [[Bank of Unification|Bank of Unification's]] International Compliance Office, is known throughout financial circles for uncovering corruption and fraud — a reputation built entirely on professional excellence rather than political connection.

The trajectory of Demon integration into the broader world remains one of the defining ongoing narratives of the Modern Age.
