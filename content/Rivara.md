# Rivara

Rivara is one of the most influential financial centers in the known world. A great port city of [[Eldressa]], its power derives not from military strength or political authority but from wealth — from the banking institutions, merchant houses, trade networks, and shipping operations that have made it the commercial capital of the continent for centuries.

---

## Character

Rivara is a city that runs on money. Old families with long commercial histories sit alongside newly risen trading houses; ancient banking traditions coexist with modern financial instruments; the aristocracy of wealth long ago displaced the aristocracy of birth.

Its harbor is among the largest and most active in the world. At any given time, ships from [[Velkrand]], the [[Meridian Isles]], [[Ilyndor]], and every corner of [[Eldressa]] ride at anchor or work through its docks. The city has served as a commercial hub since the earliest ages of Eldressan civilization, and its institutional knowledge of trade, finance, and commerce is unmatched on the continent.

Rivara also controls **Dawngate** and **Duskgate** — two subsidiary settlements that serve as waypoints in its broader commercial network.

---

## Governance

Rivara is technically governed by a **Magistrate**, though the position's authority is considerably constrained by the power of the merchant houses and trade guilds that dominate the city's economic life. Magistrates in Rivara succeed or fail based on their ability to work with — and sometimes navigate between — these commercial interests rather than exercise authority over them.

**Magistrate Helena Fairharbor (Human, Female)** is the current Magistrate of Rivara. A skilled political negotiator, she has maintained the delicate balance of institutional authority and commercial influence that the city requires to function.

---

## Major Commercial Interests

**House Valemont** is one of the oldest surviving merchant dynasties in Eldressa, tracing its history to the centuries before the Second Demon War. The house controls significant portions of the luxury goods and agricultural export markets and maintains an extensive maritime network. Its current patriarch, **Lord Aelric Valemont (High Elf, Male)**, is a patient and politically astute merchant prince. His granddaughter **Seraphine Valemont** is heir apparent and is considerably more willing to embrace modern trade partnerships and foreign investment than her grandfather.

**The Argent Exchange** is a younger but rapidly growing mercantile consortium founded after the Unification, composed of investors, merchants, scholars, and financiers from across Eldressa. It played a significant role in financing the construction and expansion of [[Valtiron]] and now acts as one of the continent's most influential economic institutions. **Cassian Thornfield** currently serves as its Speaker.

---

## The Bank of Unification

The **[[Bank of Unification]]** was originally founded in Rivara during the Reconstruction Era, in the immediate aftermath of the Unification, to stabilize post-war economies and establish the Unified Crown as a common currency. Rivara's existing financial infrastructure made it the natural choice.

As global trade expanded during the Age of Discovery, the Bank's center of gravity shifted toward [[Meridia]], and its headquarters were eventually relocated there. Rivara retains one of the Bank's major branches — a reflection of the city's ongoing financial importance — but it no longer serves as the institution's primary seat.

Many Rivaran financiers still consider the relocation a political slight.

---

## History

Rivara's commercial dominance dates to the earliest ages of Eldressan civilization, when its natural harbor and central position made it the most logical trading point for commerce moving between different regions of the continent.

The city survived the Celestial Wars as a center of trade, the First Demon War through commerce and neutrality, and the Second Demon War through a combination of strategic importance and the practical reality that destroying Rivara would harm everyone who did it. Wars require financing. Rivara financed most of them.

Its role in establishing the Bank of Unification in the Reconstruction Era cemented its status as the financial capital of the post-war world — a position it maintained until [[Meridia]]'s rise to global centrality shifted the center of commercial gravity toward the Freewaters.

---

## Present Day

In 300 AU, Rivara remains one of the world's most important financial centers despite having ceded the Bank of Unification's headquarters to [[Meridia]]. Its merchant houses, banking traditions, and commercial networks remain deeply embedded in Eldressan and global commerce.

The ongoing competition between **House Valemont** and the **Argent Exchange** for commercial dominance is one of Rivara's defining political dynamics — a rivalry between old money and new ambition that shapes contracts, political appointments, and trade agreements throughout the continent.

Rivara also maintains a significant voice in the [[Council of Valtiron]], where Magistrate Fairharbor represents the city's commercial interests alongside the political and military voices of [[Eldressa]]'s other major powers.
