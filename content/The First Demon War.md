# The First Demon War

> [!info] Canon Status
> The exact details of the First Demon War are currently under revision. What follows represents the established historical record as it is currently understood.

The First Demon War was a major conflict marking the arrival of [[Demons|Demonkind]] in Eryndor and the first catastrophic collision between the emerging mortal civilizations and the transformed descendants of the Angels of Gontkhael. It ended with the destruction of the Great Mountain — an event whose shockwaves reshaped the world and whose consequences defined the political landscape for centuries afterward.

---

## Background

Following the Shattering, the surviving Angels had been transformed into the beings now known as [[Demons]]. Eryndor survived the Celestial War largely intact, while Gontkhael had been destroyed and Nythorak banished into the Void. The ancient cycle of souls had collapsed. Reality had been permanently scarred.

The mortal races emerged and developed their civilizations during the long ages that followed. The Age of Dragon Stewardship saw the [[Dragons]] guide the earliest kingdoms, mediate conflicts, and help shape the foundations of mortal civilization across the continents.

The First Demon War emerged from the eventual contact between the mortal world and the transformed Demonic peoples whose own world had been lost in the Shattering. The precise nature of this contact and the events that escalated into open war are among the most debated and poorly documented periods in recorded history.

---

## The Destruction of the Great Mountain

The defining event of the First Demon War — and the one for which evidence is clearest — is the **destruction of the Great Mountain**.

The Great Mountain stood somewhere within what is now [[Eldressa]]. Its destruction during the closing stages of the war sent shockwaves across the world of a scale not seen since the Shattering.

In [[Velkrand]], the consequences were catastrophic: the **Ashfall Catastrophe** devastated both major civilizations of the continent simultaneously. Northern Goblin cities were buried under volcanic eruptions and ash flows. Southern Gnomish ports were destroyed by enormous tsunamis. Both peoples lost most of their ruling leadership within days.

The crater left by the Great Mountain's destruction became the eventual site of [[Valtiron]] — constructed there during the Age of Reconnection as a symbol of peace built upon the ruins of catastrophe.

---

## Aftermath

The aftermath of the First Demon War shaped the world in ways that are still visible in 300 AU.

In [[Velkrand]], the destruction of the Ashfall Catastrophe forced the survivors of both the Goblin Forge-Clans and the Gnomish League to cooperate or perish. The **Accord of Twin Hammers** — negotiated between the last surviving leaders of each people — ended centuries of conflict and laid the foundations of modern Velkrand.

The reconstruction era that followed in Velkrand saw the creation of the first **Clank** — mechanical beings built as laborers to help rebuild the devastated continent.

In [[Eldressa]], the war's conclusion eventually enabled the end of the Age of Dragon Stewardship. The [[Dragons]] who had served as stewards and teachers began to withdraw from mortal affairs during the centuries following the war, though the exact relationship between the war's end and the Dragon withdrawal remains unclear.

---

## Historical Uncertainty

The First Demon War remains one of the most poorly documented major events in Eryndor's history. Several factors contribute to this:

- The destruction of the Great Mountain and the associated catastrophes in Velkrand likely destroyed significant records from that era.
- The mortal civilizations of [[Eldressa]] were still relatively young and their record-keeping traditions less established than in later centuries.
- Much of what is recorded comes from myth, oral tradition, and fragmentary surviving texts that scholars continue to interpret and debate.

What is not in doubt is the scale of the war's consequences. A conflict that could destroy a Great Mountain and devastate two continents simultaneously was among the most significant events of the Mortal Age.
